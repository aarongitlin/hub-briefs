## how to read this doc

Every claim carries a confidence label. Recommendations should inherit these labels rather than flattening everything into fact.

- **[known]** — directly stated by Aaron or confirmed by actual wear
- **[inferred]** — repeated pattern across purchases and conversations
- **[tentative]** — plausible, carried over from a prior doc, or not yet tested
- **[unknown]** — requires a measurement, a try-on, or Aaron saying so

Direct statements from Aaron override anything here. Recent fit feedback overrides older assumptions. When this doc and a product page disagree about whether something will fit, believe neither: get garment measurements.

## 2. core thesis

Relaxed, textured workwear and soft tailoring for a design leader, sitting on a **UK–Japan axis**, adapted to Los Angeles weather and urban travel. [inferred, high confidence]

The shorthand: *downtown creative, soft utilitarian, texture doing the work that color and logo do for other people.*

What this is not:

- traditional menswear or business tailoring
- trend-cycle fashion
- generic "elevated basics" or quiet-luxury beige
- rugged heritage cosplay
- resortwear (this is the only time this doc will say it; the actual risk is low)

A piece should feel considered and tactile, look intentional with just a t-shirt, and stay wearable for years across office, dinners, galleries, travel, and everyday LA life. Pieces that only work in one narrow context face a higher bar.

## 3. silhouette

### trousers [known]

- Straight or gently tapered, moderate volume, clean drape
- Mid or moderately high rise (rise lengthens the leg at 5'6")
- Room through seat and thigh; structure enough to read intentional with a tee
- Hemmed to avoid puddling; a slight break at most

Avoid: skinny cuts, aggressively wide or balloon shapes, very low rise, drawstring-first silhouettes, anything needing major recutting.

### jackets [known]

- Chore, baker, overshirt, short work jacket, or unstructured soft tailoring
- Compact body length; natural or minimally padded shoulder
- Slightly boxy, not engulfing; works open over a t-shirt
- Patch pockets and practical details preferred over sharp lapels

Avoid: long or dropped silhouettes, padded corporate shoulders, conventional suit jackets, anything whose shoulders would need rebuilding.

### proportion principle

Controlled jacket length + correct shoulder + decent rise + clean hem = the whole game. Excess fabric below the knee is the most common failure mode for a shorter frame.

## 5. fabric

Texture is the primary source of visual interest — often more important than color. [known]

Responds well to: linen and linen blends, slub weaves, herringbone, garment-dyed cloth, brushed cotton, nep and irregular yarns, soft washed wool, micro-patterns that read as texture at conversational distance.

**Warm-weather rule** [inferred]: fiber percentage is not breathability. Evaluate weight, weave openness, lining, and density. The classic trap is a dense cotton-linen herringbone marketed on texture that wears like canvas in 90°F humidity. For hot travel: trousers are the thermal priority; the jacket only needs to survive evenings and interiors, so it can be slightly more substantial (unlined or half-lined).

## 8. fit history and alteration rules

**Worth doing**: hemming, minor waist work, modest taper below the knee, sleeve shortening, small body refinements. [known]

**Not worth doing**: shoulder reconstruction, major jacket shortening, changing rise, recutting seat/thigh, or buying anything on the theory that "a tailor can fix it." [known]

**Fit priority order**: shoulders → jacket length → rise → seat/thigh → chest and mobility → waist → sleeve → trouser length → taper.

Every sizing recommendation must classify the gap as: easy alteration / expensive alteration / structurally wrong. Structurally wrong is a no regardless of how good the fabric is.

## 9. active brief: warm-weather set (summer 2026)

**Use case** [known]: Istanbul in August (hot, humid, hills, cobblestones), Washington DC in September, then ongoing LA use.

Formula: breathable trousers + t-shirt + walkable sneakers by day; add a matching or compatible chore/baker/soft jacket for dinner.

Requirements:

- Trousers genuinely breathable (open-weave linen or linen-rich; verify weight, not just fiber)
- Jacket unlined or partially lined; evening-duty only
- Straight or gently tapered; textured; must not read as summer suiting
- Target colors: weathered mid grey, olive, dark moss, charcoal, tobacco
- Matching set is a bonus; strong separates beat a weak set
- Must survive re-entry into the LA wardrobe after the trip

Leading directions: UW linen slub set (grey or olive), compact-proportioned Toast linen separates, Barena linen, or a Japan-axis alternative if it beats those on heat performance or proportion.

Classify every candidate: (1) ideal for sustained daytime heat, (2) good for evenings/moderate heat, (3) aesthetically right but thermally wrong, (4) wrong for the brief.

## 10. evaluation standard for recommendations

In rough priority order (no fake percentage weights — judgment, stated openly):

1. Heat/humidity performance, for the current brief
2. Fit and proportion confidence, with evidence
3. Aesthetic fit against the thesis
4. Versatility beyond the trip
5. Construction and longevity
6. Value, tailoring practicality

**Required evidence for a serious recommendation**: fiber and fabric weight, garment measurements, model height and worn size, current price and stock, direct link, owner reports where findable, likely alterations, and an explicit confidence level. Admit gaps instead of papering over them.

**Preferred output shape**: buy this first → runner-up → lower-risk fallback → what to avoid → sizing and alterations → why this improves the wardrobe. Preserve the possibility that the existing favorite is still the answer.

## 11. anti-patterns

Do not recommend something merely because it is linen, expensive, European, niche, on sale, or worn well by a tall model. Downgrade: oversized minimalist uniforms, bland quiet luxury, business-casual separates, hot-weather claims unsupported by construction, anything duplicating the wardrobe without adding function or range, and anything requiring extensive justification to feel like his.
