# Commerce Brief: 2026-08-17 to 2026-08-24

## Items (max 3)
### Amazon asks the full Ninth Circuit to reverse the ruling that let AI agents shop on its site
- What happened: On August 19, Amazon petitioned for en banc rehearing of the August 4 Ninth Circuit panel ruling that had vacated an injunction against Perplexity's Comet browser shopping on Amazon.com. Amazon's filing directly disputes the panel's "the user is the one accessing the site" logic, arguing instead that "the assistant will access Amazon regardless, acting under Perplexity's control," and asks for review by 11 judges instead of three. ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417355/amazon-presses-to-ban-perplexity-shopping-agent.html))
- So what for Shopping Ads: The legal question at stake, whether a platform can gate autonomous browser agents at all, determines whether any future ambient shopping surface built on top of video (an agent parsing a review video and buying the product shown) needs a negotiated partnership or can simply act on a user's behalf. A win for Amazon here strengthens every platform's hand, including YouTube's, to require explicit agent partnerships rather than tolerate uninvited scraping and checkout.
- Thesis signal: COMPLICATES — the platform most exposed to ambient agent commerce is actively fighting to re-close the door the courts just opened, showing gatekeeping intensifying rather than receding as agents mature.
- Horizon: RESPOND-NOW
- Confidence: Single trade-press source for the filing's contents (MediaPost); the underlying August 4 ruling it responds to is separately verified via the primary court opinion already covered last week.

### Target's AI-platform traffic is growing 3.5x faster than the industry, and still "small in total"
- What happened: On Target's August 19 Q2 earnings call, CEO Michael Fiddelke said traffic from external AI platforms like ChatGPT and Gemini is "growing more than three and a half times the industry as compared to a year ago," while explicitly qualifying that it remains "still small in total today." ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/target-touts-traffic-jump-from-ai-platforms-sales-climb/))
- So what for Shopping Ads: This is the second major retailer in three weeks (after Etsy's sub-1% agent-traffic disclosure) to pair a headline growth stat with a direct admission of near-zero absolute scale, and the traffic itself still resolves to a destination site rather than a purchase completed inside the AI surface. Any internal projection for how much video-adjacent agent shopping to expect on YouTube should weight the "small in total" admission over the growth-rate headline.
- Thesis signal: COMPLICATES — fast percentage growth off a near-zero base is consistent with agent-mediated shopping staying marginal for years, and the traffic pattern described is still destination-bound, not ambient or in-content.
- Horizon: FUTURECAST
- Confidence: Primary source (earnings call quote); single company data point, though directionally consistent with Etsy's already-covered disclosure rather than an outlier.
