### Etsy's CEO says AI agent traffic "barely matters yet" — under 1% of total visits
- What happened: On Etsy's Q2 2026 earnings call (Aug 6), CEO Kruti Patel Goyal disclosed that traffic from AI agent platforms remains below 1% of Etsy's total traffic, though it converts at a higher rate and larger average order size than the rest of the site. She said most companies are overfocused on agentic shopping traffic given how small it still is, and that Etsy's real AI gains this quarter came from internal search and personalization, not outside agent referrals. [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-etsy-beats-q2-2026-estimates-shares-fall-63-93CH-4842830) / [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/etsy-three-way-ai-bet-starting-pay-off/)
- So what for Shopping Ads: This is one of the only hard, company-reported volume numbers (versus survey or vendor projections) on agent-driven purchasing, and it says the ambient/agent funnel is still trace-level even at a marketplace built for AI-platform integrations — the money today is still in owned discovery surfaces, not agent hand-off.
- Thesis signal: UNDERMINES — a marketplace CEO with the incentive to talk up agentic commerce instead says it "barely matters yet"; the volume gap between agentic-commerce hype and agentic-commerce reality is the story.
- Horizon: RESPOND-NOW
- Confidence: Single company, single quarter, no percentage given beyond "under 1%" — treat as directional, not a market-wide read.

### Court rules AI shopping agents count as the user, not the platform — Amazon loses its blocking leverage
- What happened: On Aug 4, the Ninth Circuit vacated an injunction that had barred Perplexity's Comet browser agent from shopping on Amazon on users' behalf, holding that when an agent acts on a user's instruction, it's the user — not Perplexity — who "accesses" Amazon's servers under the CFAA. [Cooley legal analysis](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2026/2026-08-06-ninth-circuit-rules-on-ai-agent-access-to-third-party-websites-under-cfaa) / [Wilson Sonsini](https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/ninth-circuit-addresses-cfaa-and-agentic-ai-tools-in-groundbreaking-decision.html) / [court-tracking blog with opinion](https://blog.ericgoldman.org/archives/2026/08/ninth-circuit-lifts-restrictions-on-agentic-ai-accessing-amazon.htm)
- So what for Shopping Ads: Any platform's ability to technically or legally wall off third-party agents from its shopping surfaces just got weaker circuit-wide — worth a policy conversation now about whether YouTube wants to gate or embrace agent access to Shopping surfaces before an outside agent forces the question, the way Perplexity forced it on Amazon.
- Thesis signal: COMPLICATES — it strengthens the legal footing for agent-mediated commerce in general, but the ruling is about destination-marketplace scraping, not content/video moments becoming shopping moments, so it says nothing about whether ambient triggers materialize.
- Horizon: FUTURECAST
- Confidence: Multi-sourced (several law-firm client alerts plus the underlying opinion); Amazon is expected to seek further review, so the legal status isn't fully settled.
