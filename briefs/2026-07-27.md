### Michaels' Google-built chat assistant converts twice as well as its search bar — but it's a destination, not an ambient layer
- What happened: Michaels quietly launched "Ask Mike," a shopping assistant built on Google Cloud's Gemini Enterprise, in May and disclosed results this week: 75,000 conversations, a conversion rate more than double traditional on-site search, and 27% of interactions ending in a product click or cart add. [Google's press release](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-07-20-Michaels-Unveils-Ask-Mike-AI-Assistant-Built-With-Google-Clouds-Gemini-Enterprise-for-Customer-Experience) went out July 20; [Modern Retail's interview](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/michaels-says-google-powered-ai-assistant-doubles-conversion-rate-of-traditional-search/) with the retailer ran July 24.
- So what for Shopping Ads: This is Google's own Gemini stack outperforming search inside a dedicated chat surface embedded in Michaels' own site and app, not inside any video or content stream — the conversion lift comes from a destination interface, which is a live, non-hypothetical benchmark for what an in-video shopping assistant would need to beat.
- Thesis signal: COMPLICATES — agent-mediated shopping is clearly winning on conversion, but it's winning through a purpose-built chat destination, not by ambiently attaching to content someone was already watching.
- Horizon: RESPOND-NOW
- Confidence: Single named retailer's self-reported numbers, amplified by a Google Cloud press release — vendor-interested on both sides, no independent audit of the 2x figure.

### Albertsons and P&G bet on a scripted taco micro-sitcom, not an AI agent, to sell groceries
- What happened: Albertsons Media Collective and P&G are running "Rico's Tacos," a 22-episode scripted micro-drama distributed on Albertsons' YouTube channel, social, and in-store screens, with new episodes through August. [Modern Retail's July 21 piece](https://www.modernretail.co/marketing/albertsons-new-micro-sitcom-is-a-big-deal-for-retail-media/) confirms Albertsons has no sales numbers to share yet, only that shoppers are scanning in-content QR codes.
- So what for Shopping Ads: A retail media network chose to fund produced, appointment-style narrative video over agent-driven product discovery, and is measuring success by QR scans back to video rather than by any AI recommendation surface — a direct data point that at least one major retail media budget is still chasing destination video attention, not ambient agent moments.
- Thesis signal: UNDERMINES — this is retail media dollars flowing toward a "watch a show" destination format on YouTube, the opposite of any-moment-becomes-a-shopping-moment.
- Horizon: FUTURECAST
- Confidence: Single-sourced (Albertsons/P&G interview), vendor-interested (Albertsons pitching its own retail media network), and explicitly no performance data yet — a strategic bet, not proof it works.
