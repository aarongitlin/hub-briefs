## Items (max 3)
### Consumers want a human checkpoint before letting AI agents buy for them
- What happened: RTB House surveyed 1,800+ consumers across the US, UK, Japan, and France and found real ceilings on agentic trust: only 35% overall (44% of boomers) are satisfied with agents purchasing without a human review step, 42% of US millennials would let an agent spend up to $250 only if a 7-day return safety net exists, and just ~33% would allow agent purchases with no return safeguard at all. AI tools overall (44% trust) still trail friends and family (59%) as a trusted source, and among named AI tools, Claude (23%) and Grok (21%) trail ChatGPT and Google Overviews (43% each). [Retail Dive, Aug 13](https://www.retaildive.com/news/retail-shoppers-warm-up-agentic-ai-purchases/827563/)
- So what for Shopping Ads: any in-video or in-agent checkout flow needs a visible reversal/return guarantee or an explicit confirm step to clear the trust bar — a silent "buy it now" moment inside a video is exactly the scenario consumers are least comfortable with today.
- Thesis signal: COMPLICATES — shows the "any moment becomes a shopping moment" premise runs into a hard trust floor that guardrail-free ambient checkout hasn't cleared, and that trust is brand/model-specific, not a generic AI halo.
- Horizon: RESPOND-NOW
- Confidence: Single-sourced; RTB House is a performance-marketing vendor with a commercial interest in AI ad activity, and the data measures stated comfort, not observed behavior.
