**Bottom line: nobody can forecast Aug 8–14 yet.** You're asking ~3 weeks out; skill in daily forecasts drops off past ~10 days. Plan on climatology, check again around Aug 1.

**What climatology says (1991–2020 normals):**
- Highs ~28–29°C (82–85°F), lows ~21–22°C (70–72°F). August is Istanbul's warmest month.
- Humid — feels hotter than the number. Sea ~24°C (75°F), good for swimming.
- Driest month of the year: ~5 rain days, ~35mm total. Rain, when it comes, is short thunderstorms.
- ~10 hrs sun/day.

**Conflicting info to flag:**
- "Average high" ranges 28°C (climate-data.org, 1991–2020) vs 81°F/27°C (weather2travel). Small, but it's a station/period difference — Istanbul has multiple stations (Kandilli vs Atatürk Airport) with real spread across a metro this size.
- Several sites (weather25, easeweather, meteoprog) present "August 2026 forecast" pages. **These are climatology dressed as forecasts** — treat any specific Aug 12 number you see there as fiction.
- Recent Augusts have run hotter than normals suggest; a 35°C+ heat spell is well within range and normals will understate it.

**Recommendation:** pack for 30°C+ and humid, plan indoor/shade midday, don't cancel anything for rain. Re-check ~Aug 1 (10-day window) if the trip is weather-sensitive.
