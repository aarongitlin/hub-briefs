**990v4 — KEEP 85%.** The v4 rides the older, wider 990 last — real forefoot room, unlike v5/v6. Your exposure is volume, not length. *Check:* Superfeet in and laced — pressure bar across the instep? Heel lock on the top eyelets. Toe splay. *Flip:* instep pressure that survives skipping an eyelet. *Insole:* helps, but keep the stock footbed — you may prefer it here. Macadamia Nut is dead center of your palette.

**Novesta Marathon Trail — KEEP 72%.** Runs slightly long in EU; 41 should land. The catch is vulcanized construction: volume is fixed, upper won't relax. Stock footbed is thin and flat — room to add. *Check:* Superfeet in, pinky pinch; soft vulc heel counter for slip; hard-floor walk for length slap. *Flip:* heel slip persisting with the insole in. *Insole:* clearest win of the batch.

**VIBAe ZUMA — KEEP 65%.** Minimal/low-stack category: wide toe box, low volume. Failure mode is you cramming a Superfeet in and losing heel depth. *Check:* wear stock first, sockless heel hold, unimpeded splay. *Flip:* feels unsupported after 20 min stock — then it's a slipper. *Insole:* **skip.** Bosco Khaki overlaps hard with the Dartflex suede.

**Flower Mountain Yamano 3 — TOSS-UP, 60% keep.** *Honest flag:* I don't have last-level knowledge of the Yamano 3. Brand-level, FM runs roomy in the forefoot with a snugger midfoot and 41 = US 8 on their chart — that's inference, not model data. *Check:* midfoot with insole in (tightest point), heel counter height, thumb-width length. *Flip:* midfoot binding with insole → too shallow. *Insole:* try stock first.

**Astorflex Dartflex — TOSS-UP, 55% keep.** The trap is knowing the last from your Chelseas. A boot and a laceless loafer at the same size aren't the same fit — unlined suede stretches and there's no lacing to recover slack. *Check:* sockless 20 steps for heel gap; the throat should feel almost tight; midfoot rock. *Flip:* comfortable sockless *right now* = too big in a month. *Insole:* skip.

**LUCA Terra Penny — TOSS-UP 50%.** *Honest flag:* I have no dependable data on LUCA's last; anything else would be brand-reputation guessing. *Check:* sockless heel hold, vamp pressure at the ball, whether the gum sole flexes where your foot does. *Flip:* any heel slip — loafers don't improve. Desert/gum reads more casual-American than your Paraboot/Velasca end.

**VEJA Salar — RETURN 62%.** You ordered a **full size up** from your 8, and VEJA's US conversions already skew long. This isn't a trail shoe needing Norda-style splay room, so a 9 likely means a half-size of dead space. *Caveat:* I know V-10/Campo fit well, the Salar's last less so. *Check:* heel slip before adding any insole; more than a thumb-width at the toe kills it. *Flip:* if the Salar runs genuinely short and the 9 measures like an 8.5, keep — that matches your Norda logic. *Insole:* don't let it rescue a shoe that's simply too long.

**Rank:** 990v4 → Novesta → VIBAe → Yamano 3 → Dartflex → LUCA → Salar.

**Least certain: LUCA Terra.** A true coin flip — no model knowledge, and laceless shoes have the least tolerance for a sizing miss and the least room to correct with an insole. Yamano 3 is a close second, but at least it laces.

**Batch notes:** Redundancy is the bigger risk than fit. Novesta, Yamano 3, and Salar occupy nearly the same slot — you need one, maybe two; return the weakest even if all fit. Two brown suede slip-ons plus a tan penny loafer land on top of Astorflex Chelseas and Paraboots, making loafers your most crowded slot. Nothing here replaces the Cascadia 19 — the Salar is a lifestyle shoe with trail styling, which is an independent reason to return it if you bought it as a backup. Try both loafers sockless back-to-back the same day; they're competing for one slot.
