# Ambient commerce

## Thesis

Shopping is shifting from destination to ambient layer: any moment of content
or agent interaction can become a shopping moment, increasingly mediated by AI
agents.

Evidence that undermines this is worth more than evidence that supports it.
The thesis is being tested, not confirmed.
