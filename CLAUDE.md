## Personal reference docs
- `references/style.md` is the canonical clothing/style profile for
  Aaron. Before ANY clothing, footwear, or apparel-shopping task, read
  it in full and inherit its confidence labels ([known] / [inferred] /
  [tentative] / [unknown]) — do not flatten tentative claims into fact.
- Never edit established sections from your own inference. New fit
  evidence (try-on results, travel field notes, measurements) gets
  appended to section 12 as a commit or PR for Aaron to approve. He is
  the merge gate on claims about his own body.
- If the doc and a product page disagree about fit, trust neither:
  get garment measurements.
- Check the doc's "last updated" date; if a recommendation depends on
  something marked [tentative], say so in the output.
