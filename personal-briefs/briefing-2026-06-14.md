# Daily Briefing — June 14, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**SpaceX closes day one up ~25%, the largest IPO ever.** SPCX finished Friday at $168.70 vs. its $135 offer price, a ~$1.77T market cap — an "orderly debut" that analysts read as a green light for the AI-infrastructure valuation thesis that Anthropic and OpenAI are leaning on for their own pending listings. The S-1 also disclosed a $41.3B cumulative loss since 2002 and folded in the February xAI acquisition (Grok, X, Colossus). [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html)

**Anthropic overtakes OpenAI in US business adoption for the first time.** The May Ramp AI Index (spending data across 50k+ businesses) shows Claude at 34.4% of businesses vs. ChatGPT's 32.3%, driven largely by Claude Code. Worth a caveat: a separate IDC survey of 1,000+ orgs still has Claude trailing OpenAI and Google on *depth* of use (19% "extensive"). Ramp measures who's paying; IDC measures how deeply they use it. [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropic-finally-beat-openai-in-business-ai-adoption-but-3-big-threats-could-erase-its-lead)

**The Information: Anthropic is blindsiding its own partners.** A report describes Anthropic launching products that compete with partners on little notice — e.g., asking Figma and Canva to co-promote Claude Design weeks before revealing it competes with them — plus unannounced shifts to usage-based enterprise billing. The kind of trust friction that S-1 readers scrutinize as a revenue-durability question. [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropic-blindsides-business-partners)

**An Anthropic/OpenAI accounting dispute is becoming an IPO flashpoint.** Anthropic books *gross* revenue (it claims principal status in cloud-partner deals); OpenAI books *net* after Microsoft payments. BofA pegs Anthropic's 2026 cloud payments at up to $6.4B — money that's revenue under one method and not the other. If the SEC forces harmonized treatment before either lists, a headline revenue figure could move by billions. [TechWire Asia](https://techwireasia.com/2026/06/anthropic-claude-enterprise-ai-openai-google/)

**China's humanoid-robot IPO wave widens.** Shenzhen's EngineAI filed confidentially for a Hong Kong listing (advised by CICC and Citic), riding a Series B that valued it ~$1.5B and a new factory it says can ship a humanoid every 15 minutes. It joins Unitree (~$7B target), PaXini, Dreame, and Linkerbot all racing to list — a domestic public-market pipeline that's starting to rival the US AI IPO cycle in aggregate. [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/engineai-hong-kong-ipo-humanoid-robots)

**A GPT-5.6 "kindle-alpha" checkpoint is leaking through Codex test paths.** Developers report stronger reasoning, coding, and — notably — a jump in vision (sharper SVG output), alongside earlier codename sightings ("ember-alpha," "beacon-alpha") and rumors of a ~1.5M-token context window. Unconfirmed by OpenAI; treat as a watchlist item, not a roadmap. Polymarket prices ~80–89% odds of a release by June 30. [Windows Forum](https://windowsforum.com/threads/gpt-5-6-kindle-alpha-leak-early-reports-on-reasoning-coding-and-vision.423497/)

**Claude Code adds `/fork` and a new CLI.** Timed against Microsoft's Build week, the update lets developers branch a session into parallel variants — pairing with the recently added nested sub-agents to give Claude Code a git-branch-like session model. [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/anthropic)
