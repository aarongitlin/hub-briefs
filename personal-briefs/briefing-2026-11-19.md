## Long Reads

**Daniel Stenberg: Actual curl rewrites in Rust.** curl's maintainer looks at agent-driven Rust ports of curl. One turned about 185K lines of C into 340K lines of Rust for roughly $8.6K of API-equivalent usage. In the same three weeks, real curl merged 300+ commits and 200+ bugfixes. That gap is the point: a port is a snapshot, and maintenance is the actual product. He also gives a useful number: only 14 of curl's 81 confirmed 2026 vulnerabilities (17%) were C-specific memory bugs. [daniel.haxx.se](https://daniel.haxx.se/blog/2026/11/17/actual-curl-rewrites-in-rust/) · [Simon Willison's take](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/18/daniel-stenberg/)
