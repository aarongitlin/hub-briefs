## Perspective

**via John Cutler, "TBM 451: What We Learn to Notice and Ignore."** When a leader says "customers first," some people instantly check that claim against the company's real incentives, and others don't feel any contradiction at all. Cutler says the difference is less about whether people *see* the contradiction and more about what it means to them. Very different childhoods can lead to the same behavior: a hypervigilant "coherence checker" or a calm pragmatist. The practical point for anyone leading a team: what looks like resistance, impatience, or indifference may come from very different places. Turning those guesses into stories about someone's character is where collaboration breaks down. [The Beautiful Mess](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-451-what-we-learn-to-notice-and)
