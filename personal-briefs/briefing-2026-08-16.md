## Perspective

**via John Cutler (TBM 436):** *Tension-Based Prioritization (A Non-Framework).* Cutler's claim is that prioritization frameworks are elaborate machinery for avoiding the decision sitting in plain view. Nobody's retro ever says "we scored that a 3 instead of a 4" — they say "we didn't have the conviction to stop X." So instead of re-prioritizing, ask why your existing process keeps generating the *same* tension. Ends with a dozen fill-in-the-blank mad libs to surface those tensions with a team; the exercise is the useful part and it's free. [Read](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-436-tension-based-prioritization)
