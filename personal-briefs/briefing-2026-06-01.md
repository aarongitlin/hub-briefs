# Daily Briefing — June 1, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic raises $65B at $965B valuation, leapfrogs OpenAI.** The new round (announced May 28-29) makes Anthropic the world's most valuable AI startup, ahead of OpenAI's $852B mark from March. Revenue run rate reportedly $47B, up from $30B earlier this year. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/29/anthropic-raises-65-billion-at-record-965-billion-valuation-promises-mythos-ai-model-in-wide-release-in-coming-weeks-releases-claude-opus-4-8/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html)

**Claude Opus 4.8 ships; Mythos teased for "coming weeks."** Opus 4.8 currently leads the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 61.4 (vs. GPT-5.5 at 60.2) and posts 88.6% on SWE-bench Verified. The bigger story is Mythos, Anthropic's next-class model — flagged for elevated cyber/coding capability including chained vuln exploitation, which is what triggered the Trump admin's policy reversal below. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/29/anthropic-raises-65-billion-at-record-965-billion-valuation-promises-mythos-ai-model-in-wide-release-in-coming-weeks-releases-claude-opus-4-8/)

**Trump administration reverses, embraces AI oversight.** Specifically driven by national-security concerns around Mythos-class capabilities. Notable reversal given the admin's prior posture; sets up CAISI (Commerce's new AI evaluation center) as the real venue for frontier-model review. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/06/trump-administration-embraces-ai-oversight-policies-it-once-rejected-anthropic-mythos-caisi/)

**Google DeepMind, Microsoft, xAI sign pre-deployment evals with CAISI.** The three labs will hand models to the Center for AI Standards and Innovation for capability and security testing before release. Anthropic was already in; this rounds out the major US labs. Quietly the most consequential US AI-governance move of the year. [Nextgov](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/05/commerce-ai-center-will-evaluate-google-deepmind-microsoft-and-xai-models/413349/)

**Cognition raises $1B at $26B valuation.** Devin's maker continues to ride the agentic-coding wave. Notable because it's the clearest non-frontier-lab AI software co. raising at this scale, suggesting the application layer is still very much in play. [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-autonomy-biotech-anthropic/)

**AMD's Venice EPYC enters production on TSMC 2nm.** First high-perf chip on the new node (announced May 21). AMD's positioning is power-efficiency-per-watt at the inference layer — and Meta already committed 6GW of AMD GPUs in February. The "Nvidia monopoly" narrative is fraying. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/analysis/amd-breaks-nvidias-ai-monopoly-5-chip-stocks-to-own-200675586)

**US closes Malaysia loophole on Nvidia AI-chip exports to China.** Commerce tightened guidance on the eve of Jensen's COMPUTEX keynote; estimates suggest hundreds of thousands of advanced chips moved through Malaysian subsidiaries over the past year. NVDA dipped 1.45% on the news. [HeyGoTrade](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/us-ai-chip-curbs-nvidia-computex-2026-huang-keynote/)

**EU AI Act gets its first amendments.** The Digital Omnibus on AI (provisional agreement May 7) defers most high-risk AI obligations to Dec 2027 — a 16-month extension — while adding new prohibitions on non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM generation. Compliance teams: timelines just slipped. [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)
