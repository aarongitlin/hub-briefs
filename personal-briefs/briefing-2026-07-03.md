## Perspective

**via John Cutler (The Beautiful Mess), TBM 428 — "Yes, Robot. Yes, Boss?":** AI amplifies the direction an organization is already moving. Where behavior is blocked by friction, clerical burden, or rigid schemas, AI removes real drag. Where it's blocked by fear, misaligned incentives, or green-dashboard theater, AI just automates the politics. Cutler's sharpest turn: the classic genie gives you what you *asked* for, but the darker genie gives you exactly what you *meant* — "help me build a defensible termination record that looks compassionate" is a prompt that works. Personal metacognition and honest systems are the only guardrails. [(read)](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-428-yes-robot-yes-boss)
