## AI & Tech News

**OpenAI and Broadcom unveil a custom inference chip.** The two companies showed off an LLM-optimized inference accelerator and committed to deploying OpenAI-designed silicon at ~10 GW scale with Microsoft and other partners through 2029 — OpenAI's clearest move yet to own its compute stack rather than rent it from Nvidia. [CNBC overview](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/microsoft-and-google-take-on-anthropic-and-openai-in-ai-coding-models.html)

**Google DeepMind's talent bleed accelerates.** Four senior researchers left in roughly a week: Noam Shazeer (Transformer co-author, Gemini co-lead) to OpenAI, and John Jumper (AlphaFold Nobel laureate), Jonas Adler (Google's AI-coding lead) and Alexander Pritzel all to Anthropic. The clustering of departures, not any single name, is the story. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/ai-researchers-continue-to-leave-google-for-its-rivals/) · [CNBC on Jumper](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/19/john-jumper-to-leave-google-deepmind-for-anthropic.html)

**The price of intelligence is going UP at the frontier.** A notable reversal: GPT-5.5 launched at roughly double GPT-5.4's per-token price, Gemini 3.5 Flash runs ~3x Gemini 3.1 Flash, and Claude Fable 5 debuted at $10/$50 per million tokens — twice Opus 4.8's rate. Frontier labs appear to be betting capability commands a premium even as open models collapse the floor. [llm-stats updates](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**Anthropic accuses Alibaba's Qwen team of a distillation attack.** In a letter to the US Senate Banking Committee, Anthropic alleged operators affiliated with Alibaba/Qwen ran ~25,000 fraudulent accounts to generate 28.8M+ exchanges with Claude between April 22 and June 5 — i.e., harvesting outputs to train a competitor. A live test of how labs police model-distillation IP. [buildfastwithai digest](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-26-2026)

**Open-source flagships keep closing the gap.** Early June shipped MiniMax M3 (1M-token context, 59.0% SWE-bench Pro), NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra (550B params, fully permissive license) and Kimi K2.7 Code (1T params, ~30% fewer thinking tokens than K2.6). Analysts now describe the US–China frontier gap as effectively closed on cost-adjusted benchmarks. [llm-stats updates](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**Claude Opus 4.8 still leads the aggregate leaderboard.** Opus 4.8 tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 61.4, edging GPT-5.5, with standout coding (69.2% SWE-bench Pro) and computer-use (84% Online-Mind2Web) numbers. [Best models June 2026](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/best-ai-models-june-2026)
