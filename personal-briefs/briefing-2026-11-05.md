## AI & Tech News

**Riot's 910 MW Corsicana lease to Anthropic is now binding, worth about $28B.** Riot's Q3 10-Q says the lease was signed Oct 28 and became binding Nov 4. Capacity arrives in three phases from June 2027 to July 2028. Base rent is $130 per kW per month, rising 3% a year, over a roughly 15-year term. One unusual clause: Riot can't voluntarily cut Anthropic's power or enroll the site in demand-response programs without Anthropic's consent. This appears to be a separate site from the $9.1B Riot–Anthropic deal in August, not an expansion of it. Bay Area Letters also reports that Google signed agreements to backstop some of Anthropic's obligations at TeraWulf's roughly $19B Kentucky campus. [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/riots-28-billion-ai-lease-gives-anthropic-a-veto-on-voluntary-power-cuts/) · [Riot 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1167419/000110465926122417/riot-20260930x10q.htm)

**OpenAI is buying Pierre Computer, the company behind Codex's diff and file-tree tools.** The deal brings in Pierre's team, its Code Storage service, and its open-source Trees and Diffs libraries, which Codex already uses. Terms weren't disclosed. The open-source projects stay available. This is OpenAI building toward hosting your code, not just writing it, which puts Codex closer to competing with GitHub. [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/openai-to-acquire-pierre-to-expand-codex-developer-infrastructure-5000565)

**Nvidia may fill only about 1% of China's RTX Pro 5500 orders.** Bloomberg (via Bay Area Letters) reports roughly six million units ordered by Chinese cloud and AI firms, worth about $70B. Nvidia is expected to ship a tiny share of them. Export controls are still the cap on Nvidia's China revenue, whatever the demand.

**Anthropic used Claude to help reconstruct a protein "vault" particle.** Per Bay Area Letters, Claude helped with DNA design, purification and microscopy analysis, and the team got an initial 3D model 34 hours after data processing began. It's the latest in Anthropic's run of wet-lab biology demos. Treat it as a lab write-up, not a peer-reviewed result.
