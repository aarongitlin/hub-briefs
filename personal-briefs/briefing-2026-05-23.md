# Daily Briefing — Saturday, May 23, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**OpenAI confidentially files for an IPO.** As of Friday, OpenAI submitted its S-1 to the SEC with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading. At its current $852B private valuation, a list near $1T would be the largest tech IPO in history. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/21/ai-news-cycle-openai-anthropic-spacex)

**Anthropic on track for its first profitable quarter.** Q2 revenue is projected at $10.9B with ~$559M operating profit, and the company has told bankers it's open to a public listing by year-end (October window most discussed). Profitability while still training frontier models reframes the whole IPO narrative for the lab pair. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/21/ai-news-cycle-openai-anthropic-spacex)

**Anthropic signs $45B compute deal with SpaceX.** $1.25B/month through May 2029 — a sign that the compute landgrab has metastasized beyond the usual hyperscaler triumvirate, and that Starlink-adjacent infra is now a serious AI vendor. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/05/21/ai-news-cycle-openai-anthropic-spacex)

**Google I/O drops Gemini 3.5 Flash + Omni, and a full Gemini redesign.** 3.5 Flash reportedly beats 3.1 Pro on coding/agentic benchmarks at Flash speeds; Omni is a multimodal-in, video-out generation family. The whole UI got rebuilt under a new design language called "Neural Expressive." [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-io-2026-all-our-announcements/) · [9to5Google recap](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/19/google-io-2026-news/)

**OpenAI launches a self-serve Ads Manager inside ChatGPT.** The platform lets advertisers create and optimize campaigns directly in the assistant. Reporting pegs the internal target at $2.5B ad revenue this year and $100B annually by 2030 — a big tonal shift for a product that has so far been ad-free. [MarketingProfs](https://www.marketingprofs.com/opinions/2026/54786/ai-update-may-15-2026-ai-news-and-views-from-the-past-week)

**OpenAI says a reasoning model autonomously cracked an 80-year-old geometry problem.** Light on details so far — worth watching for whether independent mathematicians confirm the proof holds and whether the model's chain of reasoning is publishable. [TechStartups recap](https://techstartups.com/2026/05/22/top-tech-news-today-may-22-2026/)

**Thinking Machines unveils "interaction models."** Mira Murati's lab is pitching real-time multimodal systems that continuously parse audio/video/text and respond dynamically — positioning against the turn-based assistant paradigm. Still pre-product, but the first concrete thing the company has shown. [MarketingProfs](https://www.marketingprofs.com/opinions/2026/54786/ai-update-may-15-2026-ai-news-and-views-from-the-past-week)

## Long Reads

**The Inference Shift — Ben Thompson, Stratechery.** Thompson's argument: the agentic era flips compute economics from training-dominated to inference-dominated, and the winners look very different. Frames Cerebras's IPO and Amazon's positioning through that lens. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-inference-shift/)

**Data Center Discontent — Ben Thompson, Stratechery.** Sharp piece on the growing local backlash to AI data centers. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/data-center-discontent-understanding-the-opposition-fixing-the-problem/)
