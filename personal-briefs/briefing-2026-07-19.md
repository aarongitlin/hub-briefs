## Perspective

**via Venkatesh Rao (Contraptions):** "Can the (Social) Oobleck Dance?" — Rao models society as a non-Newtonian fluid (oobleck): rigid under sudden shock, liquid under slow pressure. The teaser thesis is that "temporal forcing of non-Newtonian precommodified ooze in the appropriate context tanks might lead to prescience" — i.e., rhythmically stressing social systems at the right tempo might make them collectively foresightful rather than reactive. ## AI & Tech News

**Thinking Machines ships Inkling, now the leading US open-weights model.** Mira Murati's lab released its first model: a 975B-parameter MoE (41B active), multimodal, 1M context, Apache 2.0, debuting at 41 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — 3 points above Nemotron 3 Ultra, the previous US open-weights leader. It's positioned as a fine-tuning substrate for Tinker, a bet against one-size-fits-all AI. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/thinking-machines-amps-up-its-bet-against-one-size-fits-all-ai-with-its-first-open-model-inkling/) / [Thinking Machines](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/introducing-inkling/)

**Moonshot's Kimi K3 tops Frontend Code Arena — beating Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol.** The 2.8T-parameter model (1M context, ~1.8% experts active per token) scored 1,679 vs Claude's 1,631, winning six of seven sub-domains; full open weights land July 27, which would make it the largest open-weight model ever. [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/moonshot-releases-2-8-trillion-parameter-kimi-k3) / [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems)

**Gemini 3.5 Pro delayed months; Alphabet sheds ~$200B.** Coding performance fell short of internal expectations despite a late-June data refresh, pushing the flagship past its planned June release and dropping Alphabet ~4% in a session. The worry crystallizing: Google may be winning distribution while losing the most-capable-model race. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/16/alphabet-stock-gemini-3-5-pro-ai.html)

**Hassabis proposes a FINRA-style frontier AI standards body.** The DeepMind CEO wants a US-led, industry-funded, federally supervised org reviewing frontier models up to 30 days pre-release for cyber, bio, and deception risks — voluntary at first, potentially mandatory for US deployment later, operational before year-end. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-us-led-ai-standards-body.html) / [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/14/deepmind-ceo-calls-for-an-independent-standards-body-to-regulate-frontier-ai/)
