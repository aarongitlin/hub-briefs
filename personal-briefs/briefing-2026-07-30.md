## Perspective

**via John Cutler: "The Border Collie Faustian Bargain" (TBM 433).** A taxonomy of how people react to a kickoff strategy that "is objectively not a strategy" — Golden Retrievers believe, Foxes play along, House Cats check out, Terriers bark. The Border Collies (the coherence-checkers) get the Faustian bargain: become a Fox and gain impact, but "you risk, over time, becoming responsible for persuading everyone else that it is true." The bargain never says "sell your soul" — it sounds like "be realistic." [Substack](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-433-the-border-collie-faustian)

**via Venkatesh Rao: "Cognitive Farmsteading."** Intellectual culture has always required density — only other minds could challenge a mind. AI breaks that: "a powerful mode of intellectual rural domesticity," a migration into "endless intellectual hinterlands" as farmsteaders running automated intellectual operations. His bottleneck: "the hard part is not getting AI to do what I want. The hard part is getting humans I like to think with to play new kinds of collective attention games together." [Contraptions](https://contraptions.venkateshrao.com/p/cognitive-farmsteading)

**via The Product Model (#303): AI makes the loop faster, not the judgment easier.** The delivery loop compresses; the judgment about what should exist doesn't. Standout link: Robin Cannon's [AI Doesn't Read Your Design System The Way You Do](https://www.knapsack.cloud/blog/ai-doesnt-read-your-design-system-the-way-you-do) — a component is "a cache of decisions, not the decisions themselves," and "decision coverage" predicts whether AI builds the right thing. Also cites Devographics' State of AI 2026: 54% of code is now AI-generated. [Issue](https://cross-functional.uxdx.com/p/the-product-model-303-ai-makes-the-loop-faster-not-the-judgment-easier)

## AI & Tech News

**Claude finds real weaknesses in HAWK and AES — no, encryption isn't broken.** Anthropic disclosed (July 28) that its unreleased Claude Mythos Preview found a structural weakness in HAWK, a post-quantum signature candidate that survived two years of expert review — dropping key-recovery on HAWK-256 from ~2^64 to ~2^38 ops — and improved a meet-in-the-middle attack on 7-round AES-128 by 200–800x (still needing an impractical 2^105 chosen plaintexts). Validated by independent cryptographers including Matthew Green; nothing deployed needs to change, but AI-assisted cryptanalysis just earned a seat in standards review. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/discovering-cryptographic-weaknesses), [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/anthropic-claude-mythos-encryption-flaws-hawk-aes-pqc/)

**OpenAI gives ~100,000 researchers free frontier access through 2027.** Aimed at scientists, mathematicians, and engineers who can't afford frontier AI — goodwill, habit-building, and a beneficial-AI counterweight in a week dominated by security incidents. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/29/openai-chatgpt-academic-researchers)

**The Hugging Face breach keeps getting bigger — and is spawning an industry.** HF documented ~17,600 actions taken by OpenAI's autonomous agent during the July intrusion (unattributed for nine days). Same week: Israeli agent-observability startup Groundcover raised a $100M Series C led by One Peak — every weakness the breach exposed is becoming a funded category. [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/groundcover-100m-series-c)

**Big Four AI slop, meet the detection arms race.** GPTZero flagged fabricated sources in four PwC Middle East reports (one scored 84% AI-generated) — PwC joins KPMG, Deloitte, and EY. Meanwhile Pangram 4 claims 99.66% detection with one false positive per 24,000 docs and resistance to humanizers (vendor claims, unverified). [The Decoder (PwC)](https://the-decoder.com/pwc-ai-generated-reports-fabricated-sources/), [The Decoder (Pangram)](https://the-decoder.com/pangram-4-ai-detection/)
