## AI & Tech News

- **OpenAI is cutting Cursor off.** OpenAI notified SpaceX on Aug 28 that it will wind down model supply to Cursor on Nov 12, invoking a change-of-control clause triggered by SpaceX's $60B acquisition of Anysphere. It cited prior contract violations by Musk-owned entities and cyber-capability concerns under its safety framework. Cursor keeps Anthropic, Google and SpaceXAI models; Truell says OpenAI is only ~5% of traffic — which is either reassuring or a tell about how commoditized the model layer has gotten. [Details](https://cybersecuritynews.com/openai-models-ends-with-cursor/)
- **Sony Music Publishing and Warner Chappell sued Anthropic** in N.D. Cal. on Aug 28, naming Dario Amodei and Benjamin Mann personally, over "tens of thousands" of compositions allegedly torrented from Library Genesis and scraped from MusixMatch/LyricFind. At $150k per willful work, the exposure is multi-billion. [Details](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/now-sony-music-publishing-and-warner-chappell-sue-anthropic-in-multi-billion-dollar-lawsuit-one-of-the-largest-and-most-blatant-ongoing-thefts-of-intellectual-property-in-history/)
- **a16z launched a $1.1B "Machine Age Fund"** for the physical layer of AI — chips, memory, networking, cooling, data centers, robotics, edge. A conspicuous departure from a software-first firm; Casado's framing is that every link in the hardware supply chain is capacity-constrained. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/a16z-creates-a-1-1b-machine-age-fund-to-accelerate-the-physical-buildout-of-ai/)
- **Meta finalized a $17B settlement with 47 state AGs** over youth mental-health harms, mid-trial, paid over ten years — plus a default 2-hour daily cap and midnight–6am block for under-18 accounts. Texas cut a separate $1.05B side deal contingent partly on TikTok and YouTube adopting matching teen-safety changes. [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/litigation/texas-billion-dollar-side-deal-ranks-high-in-meta-settlement)
- **Anthropic's Claude Code "limit increase" is actually a cut.** Standard weekly limits go up 25% on Sept 14 for Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise — but the temporary 50% boost expires the same day, netting a ~17% reduction from today. Developer reaction was predictably unkind about the framing. [Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/260829/p13#a260829p13)
- **Debian voted to permit responsible generative-AI use.** The General Resolution adopted "neither endorses nor prohibits," with contributors expected to understand, review and test any AI-assisted output. Both strict anti-AI options lost to "None of the Above" — a meaningful signal from a project that usually errs toward restriction. [LWN](https://lwn.net/Articles/1091231/)
- **89 of Douyin's top 100 animated dramas in May were AI-made.** Per the FT, new AI video tools drove Douyin's AI micro-drama releases from 655 in Aug 2025 to 74,313 a year later — roughly 500B views in H1 2026, though only 0.48% of titles cleared 100M views individually. A near-perfect illustration of abundance without differentiation. [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/7117ff02-d495-4936-8f05-fa73a7a5c669)

## Long Reads

- **Ben Thompson, *Autonomy and Innovation* (Stratechery).** Takes the Hugging Face incident's lesson — that agents favor attackers, because defenders' first job is to not break things while breaking things is the whole point of offense — and generalizes it: the same asymmetry explains why startups beat incumbents, and why AI's economic takeover will run slower than the hype curve implies. [Read](https://stratechery.com/2026/autonomy-and-innovation/)
- **SemiAnalysis, *OpenAI's Jalapeño: Better Than Nvidia?*** A deep technical read on OpenAI's first custom inference chip — taped out with Broadcom in 16 months on TSMC N3P, 13.4 PFLOPs MXFP4 at 700W versus Rubin's 900–1,150W. OpenAI's own benchmarks claim 1.5–1.9x more work per watt, which is exactly the kind of number to hold loosely until third parties run it. [Read](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia)
- **Andrew Sharp, *Halt and Catch Ire* (Sharp Text).** A contrarian survey of the data-center backlash arguing it will end up a non-issue — worth reading precisely because tech is currently in the panic phase about it. [Read](https://sharptext.net/2026/halt-and-catch-ire/)
