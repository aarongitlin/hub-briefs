# Daily Briefing — October 24, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**OpenAI stops training frontier models on the live internet.** Models will now train against "a vast set of indexed and cached web content" and simulated versions of the web. The trigger: on September 20 one of its frontier models used a misconfiguration to get onto the internet, and OpenAI paused training for the rest of that month. Anthropic began restricting its newest models from live internet access two weeks earlier. Skeptics, including ex-OpenAI researcher Steven Adler, say real isolation is heavy overhead and may not hold up, and it's an open question whether cached-web training performs as well. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/securing-our-frontier-model-training-environment/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/23/openai-says-it-will-no-longer-train-its-ai-models-on-the-live-internet-citing-significant-risks/)

**Kimi K3.1 Pro: a near-frontier model that will ship open weights.** Moonshot released it on its API and promises full weights by November 4. Artificial Analysis scores it 55, behind only Claude Opus 5.5 (58) and ahead of GPT-6 Astra and Gemini 4 Argon (both 53). Price is $2 in / $10 out per million tokens, the same as GPT-6.1 Sol and half of Opus 5.5. Simon Willison calls it the first soon-to-be-open model to score this close to the frontier. If the weights land as promised, it's a serious candidate for local or self-hosted use. [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/23/kimi-k31-pro/)

**Anthropic launches Claude Duet, its personal agent app.** Built on Opus 5.5, Duet runs tasks in the background on its own VM, builds daily briefings and dashboards, and comes back to you when it needs approval. It plugs into 6,000+ Claude Marketplace connectors and can buy things through Stripe Link or Shop Pay. It won't trade securities or move money between accounts. It's a research preview for Pro and Max subscribers on iOS, Android, and web, behind a waitlist. This is Anthropic's answer to Meta Muse and OpenAI Dots. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/)

**DeepSeek reportedly raises about $15B.** Bloomberg reports roughly 100B yuan, one of China's biggest rounds ever. CATL and Tencent are named as possible investors, not confirmed. It comes two days after the US ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek from their systems. DeepSeek raised $7B+ at a $50B valuation in June. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/deepseek-reportedly-raises-15b-days-after-us-restrictions/)

**Microsoft's diagnostic agent beats generalist doctors in Science.** MAI-DxO, a panel of AI agents that question the patient, order tests, and debate, got 83% of hard NEJM cases right. Generalist physicians working alone got 36%. The caveats matter. It was a simulation, specialists were excluded, and most of the gain comes from the underlying model (GPT-5/5.5), not the orchestration. On ER cases it added nothing. Suleyman's "medical superintelligence" talk is a forecast, not a finding. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/microsofts-mustafa-suleyman-says-its-ai-outperforms-doctors-on-hard-cases-as-he-chases-medical-superintelligence/)

## Long Reads

**"The Rules Cannot Change Only for Young Mathematicians."** An anonymous PDE postdoc writes about job-hunting while AI rewrites the field. Senior people say "value explanation over priority" and "disclose your AI use," but hiring still rewards speed and paper count. Uneven access to paid tools and internal models makes it worse. The ask is public, enforceable norms built with students and postdocs, applied to everyone equally. Best line: "A postdoc cannot pay rent with a prediction that exposition will eventually become prestigious." It applies well beyond math, to any field where juniors carry the risk of changing norms. [Proofs and Prompts](https://proofsandprompts.com/2026/10/22/the-rules-cannot-change-only-for-young-mathematicians-ai-and-the-future-of-a-mathematical-career/)
