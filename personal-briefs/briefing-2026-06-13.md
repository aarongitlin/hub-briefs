# Daily Briefing — June 13, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**Frontier lab chiefs head to the G7.** Altman, Hassabis, and Amodei are all confirmed for the G7 summit in Évian (June 15–17) — reportedly the first time all three major US labs sit at that table. A marker of how thoroughly AI governance has migrated from think-tank panels to heads-of-state agendas. [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/06/12/ai-leaders-openai-google-deepmind-anthropic-g7-summit/)

**Anthropic scales Project Glasswing to 150 orgs.** The defensive-security push built on the Claude "Mythos" model — which finds and patches vulnerabilities in critical-infrastructure codebases (power, water, healthcare, comms) — expanded from 50 partners to 150 across 15 countries. Notable as a concrete deployment of frontier models on the defender's side of the cyber ledger. [BuildFastWithAI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-7-2026)

**Gemini Robotics lands on Spot.** Boston Dynamics is integrating Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6 into its Spot robot dog and Orbit inspection platform — the latest sign that the embodied-AI race is now about which foundation model you bolt onto the hardware, not the hardware itself. [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/06/12/ai-leaders-openai-google-deepmind-anthropic-g7-summit/)

**The frontier leaderboard, mid-June.** Claude Opus 4.8 (released May 28) still tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 61.4, narrowly ahead of GPT-5.5 (60.2), Gemini 3.1 Pro (57), and Grok 4.3 (53). Five new frontier models entered this spring without dislodging the top spot — the curve is flattening at the very top even as the field crowds. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**Nvidia and Microsoft want to "reinvent the PC."** Off Jensen Huang's Computex keynote, the RTX Spark Arm SoC pushes local AI models onto Windows laptops and mini-desktops (ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Surface, MSI this fall). It's Nvidia's bid to own every layer of the stack — and it nicked AMD, Intel, and Qualcomm shares on the news. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/nvidia-chases-200b-cpu-market-with-ai-agent-pcs-from-microsoft-dell-and-hp/) · [Nvidia IR](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-and-Microsoft-Reinvent-Windows-PCs-for-the-Age-of-Personal-AI/default.aspx)

**Megarounds keep coming.** Shield AI closed $1.5B Series G inside a $2.25B package (partly funding its Aechelon Technology buy), and brain-inspired-model startup Flourish raised a $500M seed. AI now soaks up roughly a third of all VC dollars — froth or foundation, depending who you ask. [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-medical-devices-futuristic-ai-gadgets-frontier-labs-mirus/)

**EU sets rules for labeling AI content.** The European Commission published a Code of Practice on marking and labeling AI-generated content (June 10), ahead of the AI Act's big high-risk provisions due Aug 2 — though a separate "Digital Omnibus" proposal is floating a deferral of that deadline to late 2027. Worth watching whether Brussels blinks. [Inside Privacy](https://www.insideprivacy.com/artificial-intelligence/eu-ai-act-update-the-european-commission-publishes-draft-guidelines-on-hrais/)

## Long Reads

**How AI firms are redesigning the data center.** Bloomberg's graphics team walks through the physical reinvention — power density, cooling, on-site generation — driving the buildout. Global data-center electricity hit ~460–490 TWh in 2025 (up 17% YoY); the IEA projects a doubling by 2030. The clearest picture yet of AI's collision with the grid. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-ai-data-center-redesign/)

**Can AI be pro-worker?** Acemoglu, Autor, and Johnson argue the labor-displacing path isn't technologically inevitable — it's a design choice, and a different set of incentives could steer models toward augmenting workers rather than replacing them. A useful counterweight to determinist takes from people who've done the empirical homework. [The New Yorker / MIT Shaping Work](https://shapingwork.mit.edu/news/the-new-yorker-can-a-i-be-pro-worker)

**Eight ways AI reshapes geopolitics in 2026.** The Atlantic Council's roundup on how human–AI interaction will press on judgment, identity, and the information environment this year — broad rather than deep, but a decent map of the geopolitical terrain if you want the forest over the trees. [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/dispatches/eight-ways-ai-will-shape-geopolitics-in-2026/)
