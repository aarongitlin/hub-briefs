## From Your Inbox

**via Superhuman AI: Claude gets Whiteboards, Sheets and Data.** Anthropic is rolling out three new artifact types to paid plans. Whiteboards are editable visual boards for brainstorming. Sheets are shareable spreadsheets, a direct shot at Excel and Google Sheets. Data (renamed from Dashboards) turns metrics into narrated stories. Claude can also now open full versions of connected tools inside a chat. [Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/more-ways-to-work-with-claude-sheets-whiteboards-and-interactive-connectors) · [Superhuman](https://www.superhuman.ai/p/anthropic-takes-on-microsoft-google-with-3-new-tools)

**via Superhuman AI: businesses can now claim a profile in ChatGPT.** Owners sign up through a Business Hub, add products and hours, and see how customers engage. Eligible companies can spin up custom agents that answer customer questions, and those agents double as landing pages for ChatGPT ads. This is a play for the ground Google Business Profiles holds today. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/openai-rolls-out-new-business-profiles-making-chatgpt-just-a-little-bit-more-like-google/)

**via Superhuman AI: Gemini 4 Carbon is generally available.** After two weeks limited to cyber defenders, Google's strongest model is open in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search, Antigravity and the API (consumer access is AI Ultra only). Simon Willison notes the API price is $2/$10 per million input/output tokens, rising to $4/$20 on Dec 1, so the current price is an intro rate. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/) · [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/10/llm-gemini/)

**via Superhuman AI: the three-tier model rule.** Anthropic's own guide says to use Opus 5.5 at medium effort as the daily driver, Sonnet 5.5 or Haiku 5.5 for simple jobs, and Fable 5.2 for ambitious ones. The same pattern maps to OpenAI (GPT-6.1 Sol / Luna / Astra) and Google (Gemini 3.8 Flash / 3.5 Flash Lite / Carbon). A common trick: a big model plans, cheaper agents do the grunt work. [Anthropic](https://claude.dev/blog/which-claude-model-should-you-build-with/)

**via Superhuman AI: why AI slop is so hard to read.** A Zonder Labs post names three faults: the real actor or verb buried inside a noun, vague wording (stacked nouns, unclear references), and statements that parse but have no findable point. Their measured "unclear sentence" rate: 78% of Claude Code replies vs 11% on Stack Overflow, and 54% of AI articles vs 10% of human ones. Treat the method as one lab's rubric, not a standard. [Zonder Labs](https://zonderlabs.com/slop)

## Perspective

**via John Cutler (Substack notes): the 4Es of cognition as an AI-strategy lens.** Cognition is embodied, embedded, enacted and extended. It comes out of people, their surroundings, their actions and their tools together, not from a brain alone. His point is that most AI strategy assumes the brain-as-network model (more data, more connections, more intelligence) and misses how thinking actually happens inside teams. [Note](https://substack.com/@cutlefish/note/c-381608827)

**via John Cutler: the Law of Inconceivable Excellence.** "The stronger your belief in what makes people excellent, the more likely you are to mistake people who wouldn't excel in your system for people who couldn't excel in any system." It's a short dialogue with a founder who "only hires A players," and it lands squarely on hiring and design-team building. [Note](https://substack.com/@cutlefish/note/c-383815232)

## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic sets its IPO range: $70–$78 a share, up to $1.404T.** The updated S-1 (Nov 10) offers 825M Class A shares, a gross raise of $57.75B–$64.35B, on Nasdaq under "ANT," with trading expected around Nov 19. Up to 20% goes to retail. Separately, AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC are buying $26B of non-voting stock at the IPO price (Google and Nvidia $10B each). Reported numbers: $81B run-rate revenue at end of October, $17B Q3 revenue, and $900.9B in compute commitments. Founders keep 50.1% of the vote through a Class F share. The valuation is up from $965B in May but below the ~$2T floated earlier. Reuters had reported a $69.3B raise that Anthropic reportedly told bankers was wrong. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-ipo-price-range-of-70-to-78-per-share-to-reach-an-implied-valuation-of-1-404-trillion/) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-sets-price-range-of-70-to-78-in-upcoming-ipo.html)
