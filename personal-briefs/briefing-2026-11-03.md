## Perspective

**via The Product Model #317: "Are We Designing Interfaces Nobody Will Use?"** Rory Madden expects about 80% of his product's users to reach it through an agent, not the UI. Notion says its MCP integration is its fastest-growing product ever. His comparison is mobile: incumbents bolted on apps, and mobile-first upstarts ate them. Agent-first products built without traditional interfaces could do the same, so your toughest competitor may have better AX (agent experience), not better UX. The issue also links an Instacart researcher's audit of his own 30-agent research pipeline, which found missing audit logs and consent failures. [The Product Model](https://blog.uxdx.com/)

## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic's amended S-1 says it "may imminently" hit its own threshold for AI-driven acceleration.** The revised filing (Nov 2) adds a risk factor: internal estimates suggest Anthropic may soon meet its Responsible Scaling Policy definition of "dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI." It also admits past acceleration wasn't spotted until after it happened. No model is designated as over the line yet, and Anthropic is considering RSP changes "to keep humans in control." An S-1 is both disclosure and marketing, but this is the first time the claim sits in an SEC filing. It goes beyond the Anthropic Institute's RSI post from June. Codex stays separate, but ChatGPT and Codex will now draw from one shared token pool, so heavy users may hit limits more often. Anthropic did the same merge of Claude chat and Cowork in September. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/openai-is-finally-merging-chatgpts-chat-and-work-tabs/)

[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/apple-restricts-dating-apps-and-ai-companions-to-users-18-and-up/)
