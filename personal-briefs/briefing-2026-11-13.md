**a16z, "Welcome to the B2A economy."** a16z argues the internet's main users are now AI agents: most web traffic, over half of new articles and most new production code. Distribution is the part that hasn't changed yet. It's bullish by design, but it's a useful map of where business models might show up next. Pair it with the Reddit move above. [a16z](https://open.substack.com/pub/a16z/p/welcome-to-the-b2a-economy)

**McKinsey, "Who wins when AI does the shopping?"** This is survey evidence on the shift from AI-*assisted* buying to AI-*executed* transactions, and what it means for brands that used to win at the shelf or the search page. It's useful data under a lot of agentic-commerce hype. [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/who-wins-when-ai-does-the-shopping-new-evidence-on-agentic-commerce)
