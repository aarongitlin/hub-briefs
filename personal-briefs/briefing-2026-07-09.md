# Daily Briefing — July 9, 2026

## Perspective

**via John Cutler (The Beautiful Mess):** "AI ROI: Same Game. Same Old Proxies." — If you couldn't measure product development ROI before AI, you can't now. Companies leaning on the same dated proxies (story points, near-term usage bumps, flow metrics that end at "shipped") will get the same results, only more conflated: AI doesn't improve the measurement model, it just increases the speed, volume, and ambiguity of signals flowing through it. Includes a cautionary tale of a "high-performing" product company whose post-layoff AI productivity story quietly turned into brain drain. [Read](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-roi-same-game-old-proxies-john-cutler-hkkac)

**Chinese models now 30–46% of enterprise API tokens:** A CNBC investigation found Chinese open-source models (60–90% cheaper than frontier Anthropic/OpenAI models) have held above 30% of gateway token share on platforms like OpenRouter every week since February, peaking near 46%. A quiet but massive shift in where enterprise inference dollars actually flow. [Bloomberg AI Race coverage](https://www.bloomberg.com/latest/the-ai-race)

**Gemini 3.5 Pro reportedly delayed to July 17 after architectural rebuild:** Widely reported (not officially confirmed) that Google scrapped the 2.5 Pro base architecture and re-pretrained from scratch — 2M-token context, a "Deep Think" reasoning layer — amid a rough week of DeepMind departures, including Gemini co-lead Noam Shazeer to OpenAI and John Jumper to Anthropic. Treat the date and details as leak-grade until Google speaks. [HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/google-delays-gemini-35-pro-to-july-17-the-strategic-play-behind-the-scrapped-base-model)
