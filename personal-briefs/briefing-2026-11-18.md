## Perspective

**via The Beautiful Mess (TBM 452): Contexting vs. context.** Cutler pushes back on the "just give AI more context" hype. Experts run on tacit pattern-matching and often can't explain it. People asking for context often can't say what they need. Each side ends up thinking the other is incompetent. Humans build shared context mostly by working side by side. So maybe AI should take part in that process, instead of us making all our knowledge explicit and machine-readable. His team's punchline: they were over-engineering AI context to avoid building it with each other. "So we just had a meeting." [The Beautiful Mess](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-452-contexting-vs-context)
