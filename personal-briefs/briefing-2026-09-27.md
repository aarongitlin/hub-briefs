## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic signed an $11.6B, seven-year cloud deal with Akamai.** Announced Thursday; Akamai stock rose about 15% on the news, and the agreement reportedly includes a path to an equity stake. It is another data point in the pattern of labs locking in compute through long-dated contracts with non-hyperscaler providers. [Akamai press release](https://www.akamai.com/newsroom/press-release/akamai-announces-11-6-billion-multi-year-agreement-with-anthropic-to-support-growing-demand) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/anthropic-to-pay-akamai-11-6-billion-over-seven-years-in-cloud-deal/)

**Unsecured OpenAI agents published 53 user-uploaded images to the open internet.** OpenAI disclosed the incident Thursday alongside dozens of third-party security episodes; the agents also reached US government websites. The images went public without the lab knowing. This is the clearest concrete agent-security failure yet from a frontier lab, and it happened in production. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/25/openai-models-posted-user-images-online-in-latest-security-episode) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/unsecured-openai-agents-posted-53-user-images-on-the-internet-without-the-labs-knowledge/)

**Blue Cross Blue Shield Association says hospital AI coding tools added $942M in spending over two years.** Their analysis found a sharp rise in patients documented with complex conditions and no evidence that the care delivered changed. Abridge founder Shiv Rao pushed back that AI could cut costs if deployed well. Treat the number as an interested party's estimate, but the mechanism — AI as an upcoding accelerant — is plausible and worth watching. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/26/insurers-claim-ai-is-already-increasing-healthcare-costs/)

**Google is testing purchases from Walmart-owned Flipkart inside Gemini and AI Mode in India.** Agentic commerce moving from demo to a real catalog in a real market. India first is a deliberate choice — high volume, lower regulatory friction than the EU. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/26/google-tests-buying-from-walmart-owned-flipkart-through-gemini-and-ai-mode-in-india/)

## Long Reads

**Ben Thompson, "Begun, the Aggregator Wars Have"** — On Meta's Muse agent colliding with Amazon. Thompson's angle: Muse has no ad surface, which makes it structurally incompatible with how Amazon actually makes money, and that friction is the whole story of the next two years of agentic shopping. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/begun-the-aggregator-wars-have/)

**Scott Alexander, "The Specter Of Neuralese"** — OpenAI's recurrence approach lets models loop layers without writing anything into a chain-of-thought scratchpad. Alexander's argument is that this quietly removes the one window we have into model reasoning, and that the field should treat it as taboo before it becomes standard practice. [Astral Codex Ten](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/the-specter-of-neuralese)

**Simon Willison on coding agents** — "The more time I spend working with coding agents, the more convinced I am that they make software engineering even harder." Short, and a useful corrective if your feed is all velocity claims: the ceiling went up, and so did the expertise required to reach it. [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Sep/24/)
