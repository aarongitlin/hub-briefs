## From Your Inbox

- **via Ben Evans: "Automation is hard" — the AI deployment company wave grows.** Following OpenAI's and Anthropic's tie-ups with PE firms and consultancies, Microsoft, Amazon, and WPP announced similar moves this week. Evans' read: enterprise AI has moved past "give everyone Copilot" (which mostly failed) — real impact means re-engineering whole workflows, and paradoxically "automation requires a lot of manual labour." He questions how different this is from old-school digital transformation. ([ben-evans.com](https://www.ben-evans.com/newsletter))

- **via Ben Evans: Meta's enterprise cloud pivot.** Bloomberg reports Meta plans to resell excess AI compute as an enterprise cloud. Evans notes the stock jumped 10% on essentially old news (froth signal), Meta has no enterprise product-market fit, and if Meta overbuilt, so did everyone — it may be "reselling into a glut."

- **via Ben Evans: Korea's $520bn memory bet.** AI-driven memory demand has hit everything — Apple raised Mac/iPad prices 20–25%, memory prices are up 5–10x — and Samsung + SK Hynix plan ~$520bn over 15 years for a new chip hub. Evans' caveat: spread over 15 years that's only 5–10% annual growth on current capex, less dramatic than the headline.

- **via Fix (Simon Andrews): Enterprise AI is built for the wrong workplace.** Bain's Saanya Ojha argues most AI products assume tech-company workflows (Slack, docs), but banks, insurers, healthcare, and government run on email, ServiceNow, and SharePoint. Buyers aren't anti-AI — they're anti-vague-ROI, anti-runaway token costs, anti-agent-sprawl. ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7477765223706193920/))

- **via HeyDesigner: 39 principles for designing human–AI interaction.** Taras Bakusevych's framework centers on one design question: how do we help users rely on AI *appropriately* — neither over-trusting nor dismissing it. ([Substack](https://syntaxstream.substack.com/p/42-principles-for-designing-humanai))

## Perspective

- **via Venkatesh Rao: Dead Forest Theory.** Rao extends Strickler's "dark forest" internet into a bleaker claim: private communities have become causally disconnected black holes, public life is either "accretion disk" artifacts or zombie performance, and AI is Hawking radiation — a fluent fossil of the pre-collapse public web. The self-reinforcing retreat means time is running out to keep any shared public reality alive. ([Contraptions](https://contraptions.venkateshrao.com/p/dead-forest-theory))
