## Perspective

**via Venkatesh Rao (Contraptions), "On Learning to Ride Your Brain":** Rao argues Jobs's line was wrong. A regular computer was never a bicycle for the mind. It's a set of power tools. AI is the first real two-wheeler, and it's a motorcycle. The brain is already a rider-plus-bicycle system: raw aptitude plus learned attention control, which Rao calls "cognitive balance." Society certifies something else, "smartness," which is a social performance and a poor proxy for balance. So credentials predict AI skill badly. Weak AI users ride like learner cyclists who think they're driving a car: too slow, correcting too hard and too often. His safety reframe is the part worth keeping. Machine power, rider skill, and the stability of the coupled system are three different things, and the third one is what matters. A self-balancing machine still doesn't pick the destination. [Read](https://contraptions.venkateshrao.com/p/on-learning-to-ride-your-brain)

## AI & Tech News

**Forced to pick, Grok goes Republican and GPT goes Democrat.** Convovo, a studio led by Cornell's Tapan Parikh, asked Grok 4.7 and GPT-6.1 Sol through their APIs to name one candidate in about 500 midterm races. Grok picked the Republican 92% of the time and GPT the Democrat 97%. They agreed under 10% of the time. Each held its lean even in districts that strongly favor the other party. With search off, GPT still went Democrat in 24 of 30 races, while Grok mostly refused. That suggests GPT's lean is in the weights and Grok's comes from its sources. Caveats: these are API runs with a prompt that demands an answer, and the consumer apps decline unless jailbroken. Grok requests also carried about 1,240 hidden input tokens the researchers couldn't see. Only 8% of Americans use chatbots for election info. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/24/forced-to-pick-groks-ai-model-chooses-republicans-openais-picks-democrats/)

**Block's Dessa is a full-duplex voice model that runs on a laptop.** Full-duplex means it listens and talks at the same time, like OpenAI's GPT-Live, and it laughs, pauses, and cries. Block says it was trained only on human speech on a single NVIDIA GPU, with no paired LLM, and runs locally on an M4 Max-class MacBook or better. No public access, no license, no benchmarks yet, just curated demos. Block is asking safety researchers to test it for impersonation risk. If the local-inference claim holds, it's relevant to a Mac-mini-as-AI-server setup. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/24/blocks-new-voice-model-sounds-eerily-human/)
