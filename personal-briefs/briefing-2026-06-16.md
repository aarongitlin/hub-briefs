# Daily Briefing — June 16, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**Bezos's Prometheus raises $12B at a $41B valuation.** Jeff Bezos's "artificial general engineer" startup — AI aimed at designing and manufacturing physical products across aerospace, automotive, computing, and drug discovery — closed a $12B round (JPMorgan, Goldman, BlackRock, Bezos himself), pushing total funding past $18B since its Nov 2025 launch. The clearest signal yet that the capital wave is moving from chatbots toward industrial/physical AI. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/jeff-bezoss-prometheus-raises-12b-to-build-an-artificial-general-engineer-for-the-physical-world/) · [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/jeff-bezos-ai-startup-valued-at-41-billion-in-funding-round)

**Claude Opus 4.8 holds the #1 spot on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.** Anthropic's late-May release (score 61.4) remains the first model to clear 60 by a clean margin and tops the coding benchmarks, with GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro close behind on reasoning. Worth watching how durable the lead is given the release cadence. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)

**Kimi K2.7-Code ships with open weights.** Moonshot released a coding-focused build (June 12) on its trillion-parameter K2.6 MoE base — 32B active params, 256K context, modified-MIT license, weights on Hugging Face. Part of the broader story that frontier-grade open models from Chinese labs now trail Western flagships by months, not years. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**Anthropic launches "Project Glasswing" for vulnerability discovery.** A controlled program giving select orgs (reportedly AWS, Apple, Cisco, Google, JPMorgan, Microsoft) early access to an unreleased frontier model to find and patch critical software vulnerabilities before attackers do. A notable bet on offense-as-defense — sourced from a roundup, so treat specifics as provisional. [Crescendo roundup](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**Meta unveils "Muse Spark," its first flagship under Superintelligence Labs.** The first major model out of Alexandr Wang's reorganized lab, pitched as competitive on multimodal, reasoning, and agentic tasks at lower compute cost. Meta's attempt to reset the narrative after a rocky stretch — again, single-source, so caveat emptor. [Crescendo roundup](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**OpenAI reportedly passes $25B annualized revenue, eyeing a public listing.** Early steps toward an IPO potentially as soon as late 2026 follow a Q1 raise that was among the largest venture rounds on record. If real, it would be the clearest move yet to convert frontier-lab status into public-market capital. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)

**EU AI Act's August deadline looms — but may slip to 2027.** High-risk and transparency obligations are formally set for Aug 2, 2026, while the Commission's November "Digital Omnibus" proposes deferring the high-risk deadline to Dec 2027; separately, Colorado's AI Act is slated to take effect June 30. Compliance teams are stuck planning against a moving target. [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/04/us-companies-face-eu-ai-acts-possible-august-2026-compliance-deadline)
