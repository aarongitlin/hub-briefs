## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic closes $65B at a $965B valuation.** The Series H pushed Anthropic past most of its peers on paper and minted seven new multi-billionaires among its founders. The pace of demand for Claude is doing more of the talking than any single product launch. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-29/anthropic-co-founders-worth-8-billion-each-after-funding-round) · [US News (Reuters)](https://www.usnews.com/news/technology/articles/2026-05-28/anthropic-vaults-to-a-965-billion-valuation-with-new-funding-as-claude-demand-surges)

**Claude Opus 4.8 ships, 41 days after 4.7.** Same price, sharper judgement, and — more interestingly — a "Dynamic Workflows" feature that lets Opus orchestrate hundreds of subagents on one task. Early testers report it flags its own uncertainty more readily. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-opus-4-8-model-heres-whats-new/)

**OpenAI launches Rosalind Biodefense and a Frontier Governance Framework.** Vetted access to GPT-Rosalind expands to U.S. government partners working on pandemic preparedness, paired with a new public playbook for third-party evaluations. Worth reading as a signal about how OpenAI plans to thread the bio-risk needle. [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/news/)

**Cognition raises $1B at $26B.** Lux, General Catalyst, and 8VC co-led; valuation more than doubled since September. Cognition says ~90% of its own code is now written by its agent — make of that what you will. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-27/ai-coding-startup-cognition-raises-1-billion-at-26-billion-value) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/ai-coding-startup-cognition-raises-1b-at-25b-pre-money-valuation/)

**DeepSeek cuts V4-Pro API prices 75%.** A permanent reduction, not a promo. Continues the pattern of Chinese labs pulling the floor out from under Western inference pricing for capable mid-tier models. [Build Fast with AI roundup](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-may-28-2026)

**YouTube starts auto-labeling AI-generated content.** The platform will tag photorealistic AI/altered video even when creators don't self-disclose — first major platform to flip the default from opt-in to detected-and-applied. Expect creator pushback on edge cases. [Build Fast with AI roundup](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-may-28-2026)

**NBA goes camera-and-AI for out-of-bounds calls.** Adam Silver announced an automated possession/OOB system on May 28, which will reduce Coach's Challenge usage. A small story, but a useful case study in how AI is quietly replacing discretionary human calls in adjacent domains. [Build Fast with AI roundup](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-may-28-2026)

## Long Reads

**The SpaceX IPO and Data Centers in Space — Ben Thompson, Stratechery (May 27).** Thompson argues the financial model for SpaceX's rumored IPO doesn't pencil out on its own, but orbital data centers — already partly proved by Colossus 1 monetization — could change the picture once terrestrial zoning becomes the binding constraint on agentic inference. A characteristically careful piece on a topic that mostly attracts hype. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-spacex-ipo-and-data-centers-in-space/)

**Too Dangerous to Deploy: Anthropic's Mythos and What Comes Next — Just Security.** A serious read on the policy ramifications of Project Glasswing — the limited release of a model so good at finding vulnerabilities that Anthropic kept it off the public shelf. Notes that at least one unauthorized actor has already touched Mythos via a vendor, and asks what the precedent looks like for the next dangerous-capability model. [Just Security](https://www.justsecurity.org/138011/too-dangerous-anthropic-mythos/)

**Too dangerous to release: is Mythos the start of the restricted-AI era? — Nature.** A complementary view from the scientific press, putting Mythos in the context of dual-use-research norms and asking whether "withhold and harden" can scale beyond a one-off. Useful counterweight to the more breathless takes. [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01617-2)
