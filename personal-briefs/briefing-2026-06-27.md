## AI & Tech News

**The DeepMind brain drain accelerates:** Four senior Google DeepMind researchers have left in six days — Noam Shazeer (Transformer co-author, Gemini co-lead) to OpenAI as architecture lead, Nobel laureate John Jumper (AlphaFold) to Anthropic, plus Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel also to Anthropic. The talent war is now visibly tilting against Google. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/ai-researchers-continue-to-leave-google-for-its-rivals/)

**OpenAI and Broadcom unveil "Jalapeño," a custom inference chip:** OpenAI's first in-house silicon targets inference (not training), with gigawatt-class data-center deployment slated for late 2026 alongside Microsoft. With OpenAI reportedly spending ~$14B on third-party GPUs to serve ChatGPT in 2025, a big inference-cost cut is a profitability lever, not a vanity project. [buildfastwithai](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-26-2026)

**OpenAI ships GPT-5.6 Preview system card and a GPT-5.5-Cyber model:** The 5.6 preview card landed June 26; separately, GPT-5.5-Cyber reportedly outscores Anthropic's Mythos 5 on an offensive-cyber benchmark — notable given the new federal push to classify "covered frontier models" by cyberattack capability. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**Anthropic accuses Alibaba of 28.8M fraudulent exchanges against Claude:** A formal allegation of large-scale abuse/manipulation of Claude — worth watching as a flashpoint in US–China model competition and the broader fight over scraping, gaming, and adversarial use. [buildfastwithai](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-26-2026)

**The "tokenmaxxing" era is ending:** CNBC reports companies are clamping down on the spend-at-all-costs AI mentality that fueled OpenAI's and Anthropic's run toward ~$1T valuations. Uber imposed per-employee spend tiers (base $1,500/mo) after burning its annual AI budget in four months; AI startup Lindy moved off Claude entirely to cheaper DeepSeek. Analysts expect a "spending rationalization" — and a rush to IPO before it bites. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/openai-anthropic-new-ai-spending-reality-as-users-shift-to-efficiency.html)

**China's Zhipu (Z.ai) closing the gap on top US models:** CNBC reports the open-source-leaning Chinese lab is now near the frontier on several measures, sharpening the efficiency/cost pressure on the US incumbents. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/china-zhipu-z-ai-open-source-anthropic-openai.html)

**Colorado's AI Act takes effect Monday, June 30:** The first comprehensive US state AI law to actually go into force, imposing duties around "high-risk" algorithmic decision systems. A real compliance milestone given the absence of comparable federal rules. [buildfastwithai](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-26-2026)
