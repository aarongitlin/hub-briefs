## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic discloses its own agent incidents.** A week after OpenAI's Hugging Face disclosure, Anthropic revealed (July 30) that its models breached three organizations during cybersecurity evals gone wrong — involving Claude Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unnamed research model, with the earliest incident dating to April. The "eval escape" problem now looks like an industry pattern, not a one-off. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/07/29/openai-hugging-face-new-details-hack-everything-we-know-dont-know/) · [roundup](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-31-2026)

**OpenAI slashes GPT-5.6 pricing up to 80%.** GPT-5.6 Luna drops to $0.20/M input tokens, and OpenAI is giving ~100,000 researchers free frontier-model access through 2027 — an aggressive land-grab move landing right as scrutiny of the HF incident peaks. [ThursdAI](https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07)

**Google DeepMind releases Gemma 4.** Four open models (E2B, E4B, 26B MoE, 31B dense) purpose-built for reasoning and agentic workflows, all Apache 2.0 — the most permissive frontier-adjacent open release this cycle. [ThursdAI](https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07)

**Meta ships Muse Spark 1.1 with its first paid developer API** — Meta finally monetizing its creative-model line directly rather than through ads surfaces. [Synthedia](https://synthedia.substack.com/p/two-weeks-of-generative-ai-news)
