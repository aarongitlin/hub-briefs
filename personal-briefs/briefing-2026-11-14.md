## AI & Tech News

**OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet, so its agents can check out for you.** ChatGPT's "dots" agents can now pick items and complete the purchase. You review the items, total and card, then approve with Face ID or a fingerprint. It's limited for now to US Pro subscribers with a US-issued Visa, Mastercard or Amex. The catch is that merchants like Amazon block AI agents, so it works where retailers allow it. That's the agentic-commerce story from yesterday, now with an actual payment button. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/chatgpts-new-wallet-lets-ai-agents-shop-and-check-out-for-you/)

**Four nonprofits sue the frontier labs to halt advanced AI work.** Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen, Heat Initiative and the Alliance for Secure AI filed Thursday in San Francisco Superior Court. The headline names OpenAI, Google and Meta, but the defendants also include Anthropic and SpaceX's AI division. They're using California's unfair-competition law plus a public-nuisance claim to try to stop work on ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. An injunction is unlikely, but discovery could get interesting, and the timing is a week before Anthropic's IPO. [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/11/13/nonprofits-sue-openai-google-and-meta-to-halt-advanced-ai/)

**Researcher says China's top open models were trained on export-banned Blackwells.** Will DePue (Unemployment Labs) analyzed open weights from DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant Group. He reports fingerprints of Blackwell training in Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4. Epoch AI estimates about 2M advanced chips reached China in 2024–25 through schemes that hid where they were going. This is one researcher's method, not a confirmed finding, but it lands right on top of the DOJ probes into Nvidia's Southeast Asian buyers. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/export-banned-nvidia-chips-are-behind-chinas-most-powerful-ai-models-uh-oh-jensen/)

**Huang's exit leaves a hole in AI startup funding.** Per PitchBook, Nvidia joined 105 funding rounds this year, often as the biggest check. VCs expect a more selective Nvidia, smaller checks and lower valuations downstream. Its $13B Hugging Face purchase is still expected to close in 2027. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/jensen-huangs-departure-blasts-big-hole-in-ai-startup-funding-vcs-and-founders-say/)

**LangChain hits $3B.** It raised a $300M Series C led by CapitalG, more than double the $1.25B valuation from about a year ago. LangSmith, its agent-monitoring product, has 7,000 customers, including Harvey, Rippling, NVIDIA and Salesforce. Agent tooling is still getting funded even as the chip money gets nervous. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/ai-agent-startup-langchain-hits-3b-valuation/)

**Notion confirms chat transcripts leaked through the Fireworks AI breach.** Notion says fewer than 1% of workspaces had Insights transcripts exposed. It's the first named downstream victim of the Fireworks breach, a concrete example of the third-party inference risk flagged earlier this week. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/cyberattack-on-fireworks-ai-allowed-hackers-to-access-notion-customers-chat-transcripts/)
