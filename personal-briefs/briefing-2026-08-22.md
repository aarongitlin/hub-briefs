## Long Reads

**"Research on AI and the labor market is still in the first inning" (PIIE).** A useful corrective to both the doom and the hype: the empirical record so far shows adjustment happening through task reallocation and altered career ladders rather than broad displacement, and productivity gains that are real but uneven — with a few cases where AI made performance worse. ([PIIE](https://www.piie.com/blogs/realtime-economics/2026/research-ai-and-labor-market-still-first-inning))

**"AI's Impact on Labor and Hiring" (Liberty Street Economics, NY Fed).** Kartik Athreya on what firms are actually doing to hiring practices and skill requirements, drawn from the Fed's own regional survey data rather than vendor claims. Pairs well with the PIIE piece above. ([Liberty Street Economics](https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/08/ais-impact-on-labor-and-hiring/))
