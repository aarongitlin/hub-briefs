## From Your Inbox

**via Superhuman AI: "Black October" is mostly a PHP story.** Two critical remote-code-execution flaws affect every PHP version since PHP 5 (2004). PHP is the language behind roughly 70% of websites, including WordPress and Drupal. AI found them: Wiz used its Atlas agent with Gemini 4 Argon and GPT-5.6 Sol, and Calif, working on OpenAI's "Patch the Planet" effort, used GPT-5.6 Sol. Patches for all of PHP 8.x shipped Oct 21, a day early, because the CVE IDs leaked on Oct 20 and Calif accidentally posted a proof-of-concept video. The NSA says to take any system you can't patch right away offline, which is why government sites around the world went dark. Superhuman's framing goes too far. The holes were responsibly disclosed, not used for an AI-agent attack wave, though exploit code has been circulating since the leak, so the risk now is real. Recommended version: 8.5.12. [Simon Willison / PHP Foundation](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/21/php-security-release/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/php-armageddon-openai-googles-wiz-find-ancient-holes-panicking-the-internet/) · [NSA](https://www.nsa.gov/Press-Room/Press-Releases-Statements/Press-Release-View/Article/4630238/nsa-advisory-php-updates/)

## Long Reads

**Scott Alexander, "Why Global Trepanation?"** Between about 10,000 and 3,000 BC, 5–10% of skulls were drilled, and many of those patients survived. He argues it worked on epidural hematomas, and the dramatic recoveries led people to use it for headaches and everything else. It faded as head injuries became rarer with civilization. It's a good case study in how a real but narrow effect turns into a universal remedy, which is relevant to AI hype. [Astral Codex Ten](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/why-global-trepanation)

**Ben Thompson interviews Craig Moffett on SpaceX and bubbles.** It covers SpaceX's launch, broadband, and mobile businesses, and how Moffett applies dot-com lessons to today's AI-capex market.
