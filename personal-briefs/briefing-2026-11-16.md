## Perspective

**via Venkatesh Rao (Contraptions): The Profligacy Principle.** Rao flips Occam's razor: when something is new enough that we don't yet know what *kinds* of things exist, we should multiply entities on purpose. Generate lots of analogies, metaphors and candidate worlds, but hold them loosely. His motto is "wild perception, disciplined belief." He argues that first-principles thinking and Bayesian reasoning only work after the categories are settled, and AI discourse is "disastrously parsimonious," stuck with a few sci-fi tropes. LLMs make cheap ways of seeing abundant, so the scarce skill becomes staying in contact with the actual phenomenon. He's also hosting a Blygger publishing-protocol hackathon starting Nov 20. [Contraptions](https://contraptions.venkateshrao.com/p/the-profligacy-principle)
