# Daily Briefing — June 4, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic closes financing at a $965B valuation, files confidentially for IPO.** The round vaults Anthropic past OpenAI in private valuation, propelled largely by Claude Code's runaway enterprise adoption; the company also rolled out a Services Track and Partner Hub for its Claude Partner Network. A formal S-1 would be the first true frontier-lab public offering. [Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)

**Nvidia unveils the RTX Spark (N1X), its first PC processor.** Announced at Computex, the 3nm Arm-based SoC ships this fall in Windows machines from Microsoft, Dell, HP, Asus, Lenovo, and MSI, and can run 120B-parameter models locally with up to 1M-token context. Jensen Huang framed it as the PC's "smartphone moment"; AMD, Intel, and Qualcomm shares fell on the news. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/nvidias-new-pc-chips-are-ceos-bid-to-own-every-part-of-ai-stack.html)

**DeepSeek nears a $7.4B raise — one of China's largest startup rounds ever.** Tencent and the government-backed National AI Industry Investment Fund are among the backers, underwriting DeepSeek's open-weight strategy that has kept Chinese labs roughly three months behind the frontier. [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/deepseek-nears-deal-to-raise-7-4-billion-for-open-source-ai/)

**Generalist AI raises $400M at a $2B valuation for robotics foundation models.** Radical Ventures led, with 8VC, Union Square Ventures, Nvidia, and Bezos Expeditions joining — another sign that capital is rotating from pure-LLM bets toward embodied AI. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-04/nvidia-backed-robotics-startup-generalist-ai-valued-at-2-billion)

**EU proposes a tech-sovereignty package and Cloud & AI Development Act.** The June 3 proposals aim to shore up European compute and infrastructure, arriving alongside the recently agreed AI Act amendments that push high-risk obligations out to December 2027 and add new prohibitions on AI-generated CSAM and non-consensual intimate imagery. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)

**MiniMax M3 ships as a notable open-weight release.** Billed as the first open model to combine frontier coding, a 1M-token context window, and native multimodal input — continuing the cadence of frontier-class open models landing roughly quarterly. [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**Microsoft debuts MAI-Thinking-1, its first flagship reasoning model.** Shown at Build, the model targets high efficiency at low token cost and reportedly matches Claude Opus 4.6 on SWE-bench Pro — part of a broader scramble by Microsoft and Google to close Anthropic's and OpenAI's lead in AI coding. [Thurrott](https://www.thurrott.com/a-i/336960/build-2026-microsoft-launches-first-flagship-reasoning-ai-model-and-more)

An early look at how slop seeps into supposedly edited publications. [Futurism](https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/analysis-new-york-times-ai-articles)
