## Perspective

**via John Cutler (TBM 428: "Yes, Robot. Yes, Boss?"):** AI can be your scribe, thought partner, or counselor — but also your consigliere, spin doctor, or propagandist. Cutler's core distinction: if a team behavior is blocked by friction, missing scaffolding, or weak shared representations, AI helps; if it's blocked by low voice safety, misaligned incentives, fear, or learned helplessness, AI won't fix it and may make things worse. ## AI & Tech News

**OpenAI pitches Washington a 5% stake, Alaska-style.** Per FT reporting, Altman has floated giving the US government ~5% of OpenAI (~$42.6B) — and 5% of every leading US lab — pooled into a sovereign-wealth-style fund modeled on the Alaska Permanent Fund. Talks are early-stage and conceptual, would likely require an act of Congress, and notably came days after Washington delayed GPT-5.6. Anthropic, Google, and Meta haven't signaled agreement.
