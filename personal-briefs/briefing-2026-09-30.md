## Perspective

**via John Cutler (TBM 442): ten career traps thoughtful people fall into.** Each trap starts as a strength — curiosity, empathy, willingness to question — and turns on you. The Volunteer Trap (you own the orphaned problem, then nobody can explain what you do at layoff time). The Canary Trap (the more incoherence you detect, the more you're consumed detecting it). The Workplace Identity Trap (you stop being a designer and become "design at X"). The useful device is what he calls the *saveable moment*: the specific, rehearsable tripwire before each trap closes. Worth reading slowly. [cutlefish](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-442-10-career-traps-thoughtful)

**via The Product Model (#312): LLMs aren't the only game in town.** TypeSafe's new "System One" models do small focused decisions — route this, escalate that — rather than generating text, so they can't hallucinate. Tens-to-hundreds of milliseconds, input pricing around $0.04 per million tokens. The argument: the sparkle-icon era is ending, because customers don't want a weaker model bolted into your app when the real ones can already reach your site. The opportunity is redesigning workflows around cheap fast classification. Their reader poll is a useful mood check too: 73.3% want the pace of AI to slow down, nobody wanted it to accelerate. [UXDX](https://cross-functional.uxdx.com/p/the-product-model-312-llms-aren-t-the-only-game-in-town)

## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic's IPO prospectus leaked, and the numbers are doing rounds in garbled form.** Reuters got a draft S-1 on Sept 28. FY2025: $4.6B revenue, $13B operating expenses, operating loss over $8B, net loss $42B. Q2 2026 revenue was $11.5B against $4.73B in Q1, with operating profitability two quarters running. The company is targeting a $2 trillion valuation and $518B in cloud and data center spending. Watch the framing — "$8B loss" and "$42B loss" are both circulating as if they were the same figure; they're the operating and net lines respectively. Also notable: roughly a quarter of revenue comes from two customers, and over a third of the prospectus is risk warnings. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/29/anthropic-ipo-s-1-prospectus-income-statement/)

**AMD is buying Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2B.** Announced Sept 28. It buys AMD a spatial/"physical AI" team and the Godmother of AI herself, against a Nvidia stack that has no equivalent bet. [AMD newsroom](https://newsroom.amd.com/news/amd-acquire-world-labs/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/amd-will-acquire-fei-fei-lis-world-labs-for-8-2-billion/)

**A cyber-capability threshold just got crossed, quietly.** Anthropic's Frontier Red Team found GLM-5.3 producing full control-flow hijacks in 4% of binary exploitation trials, and Claude Mythos Preview in 6%. Opus 4.6 and GLM-5.2 scored zero on the same tasks. Small percentages, but the jump from "never" to "sometimes" is the part that matters, and it's showing up in an open-weights model. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/glm-5-3-and-the-spread-of-advanced-cyber-capabilities)

## Long Reads

**Apps, Agents, and Aggregation** — Ben Thompson, Sept 28. Argues agents with computer access become the primary interface, which makes individual apps the commodity layer, and that volition, not capability, is now the scarce input. Pairs with his DevDay piece from this morning. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/apps-agents-and-aggregation/)

**Parsing "Can" from "Should": how Netflix got thousands of creatives to trust an AI overhaul** — First Round Review with Mckenzie Lock. They started from where workflows were genuinely broken, then used shadowing and structured dissent to sort automatable work from work where craft had to stay in human hands. The most concrete change-management account I've seen on this. [First Round](https://review.firstround.com/netflix-ai-strategy/)

**The default is not a design decision** — Shrawani Bhattarai. On how the design industry built an infrastructure of sameness and then called it best practice. Short, and it lands harder now that generation is cheap. [hipuku.dev](https://www.hipuku.dev/writing/the-default-is-not-a-design-decision)
