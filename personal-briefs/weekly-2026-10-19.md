# Weekly Brief — Oct 13–19, 2026

Demand and budget both moved this week. Holiday spending is landing in October, ad money is sliding from performance into creators and video, and the first hands-on evals of agent-built UI say the models execute well and judge badly.

## Commerce × Media × AI

- **Nearly a third of holiday budgets will be gone before Halloween.** Deloitte surveyed 4,000 US consumers Aug 26 to Sep 10: 28% of shoppers' budgets are expected to be exhausted by end of October, highest in six years, with October sales participation up 18 points since 2023. The counterintuitive part is that the 38% Deloitte calls "value seekers" plan to spend 12% more than everyone else. Deal-hunting is a high-spend behavior, so price and promo surfaces deserve premium treatment, not a discount ghetto. [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/retail-distribution/holiday-retail-sales-consumer-survey.html) · [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/nearly-30-of-holiday-budgets-expected-to-be-spent-by-the-end-of-october/)
- **A TikTok-native brand did $130M in year one and is now on Target shelves.** Mellow Sleep launched in 2025, became TikTok's top-selling sleep brand, and is pacing past $300M in year two. It entered 550 Target stores on Oct 18, Target-exclusive, five products from $39.99 to $129.99. The video-to-shelf pipeline now has a number on it, and the shelf is the back half of that funnel rather than the front. [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/operations/target-bets-on-buzzy-tiktok-brands-like-mellow-sleep-to-revamp-its-home-category/)
- **Ruggable moved about 10% of total spend out of performance into creators and CTV.** Last holiday it shifted over $1M from its Meta performance budget into ShopMy creators; that channel did roughly $3M in month one, doubled, then tripled, and CMO Lauren Sherman-Kaoud says its working media beat both traditional ads and TikTok on conversion. She plans another 15% to 20% of brand-channel growth next year. Self-reported by one advertiser, but a named CMO putting figures on a reallocation others only describe. [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/marketing/ruggables-new-ad-spend-strategy-shifts-focus-to-shopmy-creators/)
- **Amazon is using Thursday Night Football to sell ads to brands that don't sell on Amazon.** Since 2022 it has brought 80 brands new to TNF and 54 new to Amazon entirely through live sports. The mechanism is the part worth stealing: a brand that ran a TNF spot gets retargeted with contextual retail ads, closing the video buy and the commerce buy into one loop. [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media-buying/how-amazon-turned-its-thursday-night-football-into-a-two-pronged-pitch-to-capture-ad-dollars/)

## UX Practice × Agentic AI

- **The capability frontier is jagged, and taste is on the lagging side.** Claire Vo ran nearly 20 tasks against Meta's Muse Wonder. It was her favorite model on all four agentic tasks: on a billing ticket it fixed the account link, refunded a duplicate charge, changed the plan, sent the confirmation and ignored a planted prompt injection. Its frontend output was the worst she has seen recently, with blurple palettes, fat serifs, dead buttons, brand guidelines ignored and a dark-mode prototype that rendered light under an overlay. She keeps Opus 5.5 for UI work. That gap is the thing to write evals against. [ChatPRD](https://www.chatprd.ai/how-i-ai/meta-muse-wonder-review)
- **"Teach the timing; keep responsibility for the joke."** A creator with 200k+ TikTok followers replaced her video editor with Claude Code orchestrating FFmpeg, Whisper and Python locally. A five-minute phone take becomes a 15-second vertical cut in about 15 seconds: eight cuts, 57 one-word captions, zero audio offset. The edit lives in a TSV of start, end, shot size and label, so every decision is inspectable and one command yields 15, 30 and 60-second versions. It worked because she already knew what a good cut sounds like. [ChatPRD](https://www.chatprd.ai/how-i-ai/brooke-lacey-claude-code-video-editing)

## Thesis check

- **Commerce:** Strengthened at the edges, not the center. Demand is going ambient, but almost none of this week's evidence is agent-mediated. The sharpest counter came via the WSJ (paywalled, relayed secondhand): agents hurt Booking and Expedia while Airbnb may be spared, because unique inventory cannot be price-shopped. If that holds, agents commoditize the comparable and leave the distinctive alone, which is narrower than "shopping becomes an agent layer."
- **UX practice:** Strengthened, with the value in one place. Both evals say models execute specified behavior well and judge their own output badly, so writing the spec and the eval is the defensible design work. Vo's results also show that "the agent designs it" is not yet true for anything brand-sensitive.
