## AI & Tech News

**Moonshot releases Kimi K3, the largest open model ever.** A 2.8T-parameter sparse MoE with native text/image/video and a 1M-token context window, open weights dropped at 00:00 UTC July 27. MXFP4 quantization brings it to ~1.4TB, priced around $3/$15 per M tokens — undercutting comparable US frontier models and reigniting the "are the labs' moats real" debate. [LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news), [BuildFast](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-27-2026)

**Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5 and Claude Science.** Opus 5 launched July 24 at $5/$25 per M tokens and leads FrontierBench v0.1 (43.3% vs GPT-5.6 Sol's 37.5%). Alongside it: Claude Science, a research workbench (60+ preconfigured tools, beta for Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise) aimed at compressing life-sciences R&D, plus an internal drug-discovery program for neglected diseases. [ThursdAI](https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07)

**Meta's Muse Spark 1.1 arrives with Meta's first paid developer API.** A 1M-context agentic model that reportedly rivals GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.8 on agentic evals, with computer use across desktop/browser/mobile and parallel subagent delegation; API in US-only public preview. Meta charging developers is the actual headline. [ThursdAI](https://thursdai.news/releases/2026-07)

**Nvidia reportedly weighing a ~$250B backstop for OpenAI's Ohio buildout.** Talks would have Nvidia financially guaranteeing OpenAI's lease of a 10GW data center SoftBank's energy arm is developing in Piketon, Ohio — circular-financing concerns at a scale that makes last year's deals look quaint. Single-sourced so far; treat as reported, not confirmed. [BuildFast](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-july-26-2026)
