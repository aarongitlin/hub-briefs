## Perspective

**via John Cutler (The Beautiful Mess): AI ROI: Same Game. Same Old Proxies.** If you couldn't measure product development ROI before AI, you can't now — AI doesn't fix a broken measurement model, it just increases the speed, volume, and ambiguity of signals flowing through it. [Read on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/ai-roi-same-game-old-proxies-john-cutler-hkkac)

**via Venkatesh Rao (Contraptions): A Tale of Two Theories (of Coordination).** The leverage-cycle camp (massive AI capex, deficits, buoyant equities) and the deleveraging camp (CRE trouble, slowing globalization, aging demographics) are both right and both wrong: the economy isn't levering or delevering, it's *reallocating* leverage from one theory of coordination to another. Markets are deciding which kinds of coordination deserve to be financed, maintained, or wound down. Note: published in his AI-assisted "Sloptraptions" section.
