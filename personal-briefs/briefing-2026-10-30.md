## From Your Inbox

**via Lenny's Newsletter (The Skip): how Anthropic's research PM team hires and ramps.** Nikhyl Singhal talks with Dianne Na Penn, who leads product for Anthropic's research and Labs teams. She joined in 2023 as the first technical PM, and her team has run every model from Claude 2 to the 5.5 family and incubated Claude Code, MCP, and Claude Design. The first two months are built to be the hardest, with no scaffolding: new PMs read back through months of public channels instead of asking to be caught up, and she can usually tell how someone is doing within a month or two. People who do well have a story of pushing through inertia, like a founder, a first startup PM, or someone who got hard things done inside a big company. People who struggle wait for structure and defer to hierarchy. She calls out early-career PMs and forward-deployed engineers as standout hires. [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/cp/222659908) · [The Skip](https://theskip.substack.com/p/inside-pm-at-anthropic)

**via Fix / Insider: DoorDash buys Instacart for $17B.** DoorDash's pitch is that Instacart knows how to run grocery for grocers and DoorDash brings the delivery network. After the deal closes there will be one combined membership and no exclusivity for local grocers. Expect pushback from grocers and gig workers, since a major competitor disappears in a market Amazon, Walmart, and Uber are all pushing into. There's a funny AI footnote from DoorDash's earnings call: Tony Xu says third-party AI agents (Muse and others) have placed "maybe several hundred orders in total," against roughly 3M deliveries a day. ## AI & Tech News

**Mistral releases weights for a trillion-parameter model.** Mistral Large 4 ("Le Chonk") is on Hugging Face and the API, pitched at cyber defense. Mistral says it matches China's GLM-5.3 while using 47% fewer tokens per task, and that it tops other open models on Artificial Analysis' Coding Agent Index. It's the biggest open-weight release from a Western lab so far. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/mistral-releases-weights-for-its-trillion-parameter-ai-model-targeting-cybersecurity/)

**Reddit moves behind a login as search traffic dries up.** Search referrals fell by more than 1M a week on average in October, and US daily users dropped from 53.2M to 50.7M quarter over quarter, even as revenue grew 53% to $895M. Going logged-in protects the data it licenses to model providers from scraping. Tests start outside the US in Q4. It's Thompson's "end of the open internet" argument showing up in an earnings report. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/as-search-traffic-dries-up-reddit-announces-its-shifting-to-a-logged-in-experience/)

**Jassy: AWS will be a $1T business, after a lot more spending.** AWS is at a $184B annualized run rate, up 39% year over year. Jassy expects growth "north of 40%" in 2027 and 2028, and $1T in revenue in 5–10 years. 2026 capex is about $220B and goes up again in 2027. The stock fell about 6% after hours. Taken with Microsoft's numbers yesterday, the hyperscalers are growing fast and spending even faster. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/aws-will-be-a-1t-business-jassy-says-but-first-it-will-spend-big/)

**OpenAI copies Anthropic's subscriber API credits.** ChatGPT subscribers on the $100, $200, and $500 a month plans now get the same amount in monthly API credits, which expire. Anthropic launched the same perk for Max and Team earlier this month. For a home-server setup that runs both, this effectively makes API usage free up to the cap. ## Long Reads

**Jacob Sansbury, "How I Built the Most AI-Pilled Company in the World" (Oct 29, via Simon Willison).** Spawn is a multiplayer voxel game whose agents ship to production every hour, and an agent owns each incident. Sansbury says token spend runs about 30x payroll, more than $3M a month. He credits Fable 5 with making that level of autonomy possible, says Opus 5.5 "doesn't quite hack it," and describes a GPT-6 Astra security pass that fixed things by turning every feature off. Willison calls it a rare look at the far edge of agentic engineering. Read it as one founder's account, not as a benchmark.
