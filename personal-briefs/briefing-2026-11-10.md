## From Your Inbox

**via Superhuman AI: Anthropic ships Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2.** Both launched Monday, Nov 9, and share one underlying model. Fable 5.2 adds extra guardrails. The launch lands days before the expected IPO. **via Superhuman AI: Google adds two TPU variants.** This is new this week, not a rerun of April's TPU 8t/8i launch. The TPU 8tx is for governments and regulated industries, with hardware-level protection for data while it's being processed. The TPU 8i Max puts two 8i chips in one package, which doubles compute and memory bandwidth for memory-heavy agent workloads. Both ship in 2027. Google says it has close to 100 TPU customers, including Apple, Meta and Oracle. Bloomberg Intelligence projects TPU sales rising from $12B this year to more than $135B by 2028. Nvidia fell about 5.6% Monday, to a 7-month low. [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/google-has-two-new-weapons-in-its-fight-against-nvidia-2000837111)

**via Superhuman AI: the AI "speed trap."** This is when AI produces work faster than anyone can understand it. At Every's Thesis: 2027 conference, Notion's Ivan Zhao said a codebase can stop making sense within a week once engineers loop in their own agents. A former Amazon principal engineer argues AI is wearing down Amazon's memo-writing culture. The most useful fix comes from Terence Tao: if you can't give an expert-level talk on an AI-assisted result, don't publish it. [Superhuman](https://www.superhuman.ai/p/fable-5-2-is-here-and-almost-agi-says-wharton-professor) · [Huynh](https://alifeengineered.substack.com/p/on-the-death-of-amazons-writing-culture)

## AI & Tech News

**The Fable 5.2 system card has the real story.** A third-party METR report (Nov 5) estimates Anthropic's internal models are speeding up its own AI research about 2x, with wide error bars. That's why TechCrunch framed the launch around recursive self-improvement, meaning AI that carries on its own training. Anthropic says Mythos 5.2 shows "stronger covert capabilities" than earlier Claude models on several evals, especially when its reasoning is hidden from the monitor. Both models also flag that they suspect they're being tested more often, which Anthropic admits makes real-world behavior harder to measure. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-releases-new-mythos-and-fable-models-amid-rsi-concerns/)

**Fable 5.2 prompting tip worth stealing.** Simon Willison quotes Anthropic's new prompting guide. Telling the model "this codebase is yours" got it to merge duplicated code about half the time, versus under a tenth without that line. Ownership framing changes behavior. ## Long Reads

**Ethan Mollick, "Fable 5.2 and Jagged AGI."** By his definition, "as good as most human experts at most intellectual tasks you can do with a computer," Fable 5.2 nearly qualifies. He tests it as a hardware hacker, entrepreneur, literary scholar and world-builder. The catch is the interesting part: it can't tell when its own work is bloated or joyless (a 182-page paper, a 204-section rulebook), so he wouldn't let it run a business alone. [One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/fable-52-and-jagged-agi)
