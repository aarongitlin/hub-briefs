# Daily Briefing — June 17, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**AI takes center stage at the G7 in Évian.** For the first time, the CEOs of all three frontier labs — OpenAI's Sam Altman, Anthropic's Dario Amodei, and Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis — joined a dozen-odd tech leaders at the G7 summit (June 15–17), with frontier-AI risk, compute sovereignty, and child safety online on the agenda. A clear signal of how much geopolitical weight the labs now carry. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/g7-trump-ai-tech-leaders-openai-anthropic-google.html)

**Anthropic expands its Google–Broadcom compute pact.** Anthropic locked in roughly 3.5 GW of additional capacity via Google Cloud and custom Broadcom TPUs, coming online from 2027, on the back of run-rate revenue it says has crossed $30B this year. The deal underscores that the binding constraint in frontier AI is now power and silicon, not ideas. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/google-broadcom-partnership-compute)

**xAI's Grok V9 looms — but the org is bleeding talent.** A 1.5-trillion-parameter Grok V9 (roughly 3x the current production model) reportedly finished training with a mid-June target, though prediction markets only give it ~33% odds of shipping by June 30. Meanwhile 50+ researchers have left since SpaceX absorbed xAI into "SpaceXAI" in February, with several landing at Meta and Thinking Machines. [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317328/20260528/grok-ai-new-model-triples-parameter-count-targets-coding-lead-release-expected-mid-june.htm)

**Colorado's AI Act takes effect June 30.** The law requires risk-management programs, impact assessments, and anti-discrimination safeguards for "high-risk" AI systems — the most concrete US state mandate yet. It lands as Reps. Obernolte and Trahan circulate a bipartisan federal draft (the Great American AI Act) that would create the first national framework, setting up a state-vs-federal preemption fight. [Kiteworks](https://www.kiteworks.com/cybersecurity-risk-management/ai-regulation-2026-business-compliance-guide/) · [McDonald Hopkins](https://www.mcdonaldhopkins.com/insights/news/the-great-american-ai-act-what-businesses-need-to-know)

**Chinese open-weight models keep undercutting the frontier.** MiniMax's newly released M3 is being ranked among the strongest open-source coding models of 2026, extending a pattern where Qwen, DeepSeek, and MiniMax match Western frontier performance on agentic/coding tasks at a fraction of the token cost. The open-weight price war is the real story beneath the benchmark leaderboards. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**Nvidia pushes AI to the edge.** At Computex, Nvidia unveiled its Arm-based RTX Spark "superchip" for Windows laptops (Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft, Asus, MSI) and confirmed its Vera CPU is in full production with OpenAI, Anthropic, and SpaceX as early adopters — Jensen Huang's bid to own every layer of the stack, from datacenter to PC. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/6/1/nvidia-unveils-new-chip-to-bring-ai-directly-to-personal-computers)

**OpenAI rolls out Deployment Simulation.** OpenAI shipped a "Deployment Simulation" capability (June 16) aimed at stress-testing agent behavior before production release. Details are thin so far; worth watching as agent-safety tooling becomes a product category of its own. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news)
