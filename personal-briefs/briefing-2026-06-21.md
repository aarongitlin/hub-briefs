**via Venkatesh Rao (Contraptions):** "A Camera, Not an Engine II" extends his running metaphor — generative AI as a *camera* you point at latent space to capture an image, rather than an *engine* that does work — now pushed into the agentic era. The interesting move is asking what "pointing and shooting" means when the thing you're aiming is itself autonomous. [Link](https://contraptions.venkateshrao.com/)

## AI & Tech News

**John Jumper leaves Google DeepMind for Anthropic.** The AlphaFold co-creator and 2024 Nobel laureate announced his exit (June 19) after nearly nine years at DeepMind. It lands the same week Noam Shazeer reportedly left Google for OpenAI — a real talent-gravity shift toward the labs — while Polymarket has Anthropic going public by end of 2026 at roughly 74%. [Source](https://explainx.ai/blog/john-jumper-leaves-google-deepmind-anthropic-alphafold-2026)

**EU picks the EUROPA consortium for its Frontier AI Grande Challenge.** The European Commission named its winner (June 19) for an open-source frontier model spanning all 24 EU languages — Europe's clearest swing yet at sovereign, non-US/China model capacity. [Source](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)

**The US–China model gap keeps narrowing on cost.** Alibaba's Qwen 3.7 Max and the newly shipped MiniMax M3 are matching Western flagships on agentic benchmarks at a fraction of the price — roughly half the input and a quarter of the output cost vs. comparable Claude tiers — alongside a busy open-source fortnight (NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra, Kimi K2.7 Code). The story is less "who's smartest" and more "intelligence is getting cheap." [Source](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/june-2026-ai-launch-wave/)

## Long Reads

**Stratechery — "AI and the Human Condition" (Ben Thompson).** Argues that in a world of advanced robotics and AI, the historical self-correcting feedback between labor and capital breaks down, with uncomfortable implications for inequality. One of Thompson's more philosophical pieces, less strategy-memo than usual. [Read](https://stratechery.com/2026/ai-and-the-human-condition/)

**Astral Codex Ten — Scott Alexander on the pace of progress.** Frames just how compressed the technology curve has gotten (more discovered in 2025–26 than in entire ancient centuries) and sharpens his definition of the "point of no return" — the moment humans would lose any plausible ability to stop a misaligned AI. [Read](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/my-ai-opinions)

**Asterisk — revisiting "What 2026 Looks Like."** A retrospective on Daniel Kokotajlo's 2021 essay (the precursor to AI 2027), now that we're actually living in 2026 — and how unnervingly close several of his calls landed. Good calibration check on whose forecasts to trust. [Read](https://asteriskmag.substack.com/p/before-he-wrote-ai-2027-he-predicted)
