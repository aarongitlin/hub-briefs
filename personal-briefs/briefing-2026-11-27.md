## From Your Inbox

**via Superhuman: Nvidia pauses server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries.** Per the WSJ, Nvidia told partners including Dell and Super Micro to stop shipping servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia for now. It wants to check whether partners there meet new US export license rules. The trigger: Chinese firms reportedly rent Nvidia compute remotely from data centers in the region. The Institute for Progress estimates those remote centers supply about 24% of China's AI compute. Fitch expects data-center investment in the region to slow and get more selective. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/nvidia-hits-pause-on-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia/) · [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-chip-curbs-distillation-crackdown-threaten-chinese-ai-model-development-2026-11-26/) · [Fitch](https://www.fitchratings.com/research/banks/us-chip-controls-to-reshape-asia-data-centre-financing-26-11-2026)

**via Superhuman: 42 state AGs fight Character.AI's plan to sell users' chats in bankruptcy.** The dataset covers chat histories from 21M+ US accounts, 2.1M of them tied to minors, plus IP addresses. The only disclosed bidder, Smirks and Pangs Acquisition LLC, was formed in Delaware on Nov 13. The auction is set for Dec 1. The AGs say buyers wouldn't have to honor the old privacy policy and users couldn't delete their data. It's a preview of what happens to "private" AI conversations when the company holding them fails. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/state-ags-fight-character-ais-plan-to-auction-off-users-private-chats/) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1042620/character-ai-sale-state-ags-block-selling-users-chats)

## AI & Tech News

**1Password was breached through an employee's GitHub account.** The attacker got in on Nov 19 and pushed malicious code to internal servers. They downloaded customer names, login emails, and account creation and last-login dates. 1Password says vaults were "at no point" accessible. It hasn't said how many customers were affected. If you use 1Password, especially its agent login feature, expect targeted phishing. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/1password-said-hackers-broke-into-its-internal-systems-but-customer-passwords-are-safe/)
