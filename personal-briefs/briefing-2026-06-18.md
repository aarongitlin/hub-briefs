# Daily Briefing — June 18, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**AI bosses crash the G7.** At the G7 summit in Évian-les-Bains, Anthropic's Dario Amodei and DeepMind's Demis Hassabis pushed for a US-led coalition to set global AI rules, while OpenAI's Sam Altman called for an international forum to standardize capability and risk testing. A dozen lab and tech execs sharing a closed-door lunch with heads of state is itself the story — a marker of where leverage now sits. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/g7-trump-ai-tech-leaders-openai-anthropic-google.html)

**Anthropic opens in Seoul.** Anthropic announced a Seoul office and a clutch of partnerships across the Korean AI ecosystem — its latest move to plant flags in Asia's chip- and manufacturing-heavy markets. The timing, alongside the G7 coalition talk, underscores how aggressively the labs are courting allied governments. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news)

**Export controls bite frontier models.** Anthropic has disabled access to its newest models (Fable 5 and Mythos 5) after Washington imposed export controls citing national security; OpenAI, meanwhile, is rolling GPT-5.5 Cyber out to vetted security teams only. The frontier is starting to look less like a product launch and more like a controlled substance. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/06/17/anthropic-amodei-google-hassabis-us-ai-coalition-g7.html)

**Gemini 3.5 Flash ships.** Google's new mid-tier model claims flagship-level intelligence at higher speed, beating Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding and agentic benchmarks, and is live in the Gemini API across AI Studio and Android Studio. The "cheap model that punches up" is becoming the most competitive tier in the market. [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/google-io-2026-all-our-announcements/)

**OpenAI puts ads in ChatGPT.** OpenAI launched a self-serve Ads Manager inside ChatGPT — advertiser tooling, measurement, the works — alongside three new real-time audio models for conversational agents, translation, and transcription. The ads move is the more consequential one: it signals how OpenAI plans to monetize its free tier at scale. [Snowflake/industry roundup](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates)

**Mistral goes full Apache.** Mistral Large 3 and Small 4 now ship under Apache 2.0, dropping the company's earlier restrictive licensing — a notable open-weights concession as Chinese labs (Qwen, DeepSeek) keep raising the bar on permissive releases. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**The funding firehose keeps spraying.** Fresh rounds this month: Flourish, a brain-inspired AI startup backed by Bezos and Lux, raised $500M seed; Suno hit a $5.4B valuation on a $400M Series D; Supabase took $500M at $10.5B. Context: AI absorbed ~80% of all global VC ($242B) in Q1, and Anthropic's late-May $65B round pushed it past OpenAI in private valuation for the first time. [Crunchbase](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-june-5-2026/)

## Long Reads

**AI and the Human Condition — Ben Thompson (Stratechery).** Thompson's argument that AI-driven job loss only matters if humans care — and that if they do, they'll invent new work — with a side thesis on content as the "totem pole" around which sustainable communities form. A characteristically contrarian take on the labor-displacement panic. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/ai-and-the-human-condition/)

**New Paradigms Won't Save You — Scott Alexander (Astral Codex Ten).** Alexander on why technological diffusion needs a wide "surface area" of practitioners at each level, and what that implies for how fast capabilities can actually leap — plus his framing of the AI "point of no return." Useful as a brake on both doomer and accelerationist intuitions. [Astral Codex Ten](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/new-paradigms-wont-save-you)

**What's New in Biology — Works in Progress.** A tour through the year's most consequential bio advances: the most effective weight-loss drug yet, fresh cancer breakthroughs, gene editing for cholesterol, and ancestral CRISPR systems. A reminder that the non-AI frontier is moving fast too. [Works in Progress](https://www.worksinprogress.news/)
