## From Your Inbox

**via Ben Evans: the Anthropic S-1 is a snapshot with little predictive value.** His read of the unit economics: inference runs at about 85% gross margin, but Anthropic spent 60% of H1 revenue on R&D, and 80% of Q3 revenue came from models trained in Q2 and Q3. So the business as a whole has about 25% margins, and after hyperscaler revenue shares it barely breaks even. The "fixed" cost (training) scales with revenue, while the marginal cost stays flat. The open questions are pricing once the capex wall lands, whether bigger training runs keep paying off, and whether models stay interchangeable. His analogy is Netscape or the mobile carriers, not Cisco: being early does not guarantee you capture the value. [Benedict's Newsletter](https://mailchi.mp/6c1a426f9a17/benedicts-newsletter-1213692)
