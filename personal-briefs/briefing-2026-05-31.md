# Daily Briefing — May 31, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic closes $65B Series H at a $965B valuation, surpassing OpenAI.** The round (Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks, Sequoia) nearly triples February's $380B mark and crowns Anthropic the most valuable private AI company; revenue run-rate reportedly hit $47B. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html)

**Cognition raises $1B at a $26B valuation.** The Devin maker's round (Lux, General Catalyst, 8VC) signals that AI-for-software-engineering remains the most heavily bid vertical even as broader megaround activity cools. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-27/ai-coding-startup-cognition-raises-1-billion-at-26-billion-value)

**OpenAI publishes its Frontier Governance Framework.** Released May 29, the document formalizes how OpenAI will evaluate and gate frontier model deployments — its answer to Anthropic's RSP and a notable artifact ahead of expected US policy moves. [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/news/)

**OpenAI ships GPT-5.5 Instant refresh, sunsets o3 and GPT-4.5.** The May 28 update reworks GPT-5.5 Instant's style (less bullet-heavy, more natural pacing) and removes Canvas in favor of in-chat writing/code blocks; o3 retires Aug 26, GPT-4.5 on Jun 27. [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)

**NVIDIA unveils next-gen chips aimed at 6G networks.** Positioned as an AI-native silicon line for telecom infrastructure, the announcement extends Nvidia's land grab beyond datacenters into carrier-grade compute. [Voice of Emirates](https://www.voiceofemirates.com/en/science-and-tech/2026/05/31/nvidia-unveils-next-gen-chips-to-bolster-6g-networks-and-future-communication-efficiency/)

**Huawei readies "LogicFolding" Kirin chips for fall launch.** China's workaround approach to advanced-node manufacturing under US sanctions is materializing in product form, with implications for Apple's China share and the broader export-control regime. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/25/huawei-chip-logicfolding-semiconductor-nvidia-china.html)

**EU AI Act timelines slip; new prohibitions added.** Negotiators agreed May 7 to defer high-risk system obligations to Dec 2027 and synthetic-content transparency to Dec 2026, while adding hard prohibitions on non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM generation. Formal adoption is expected in June. [Global Policy Watch](https://www.globalpolicywatch.com/2026/05/eu-ai-act-update-timeline-relief-targeted-simplification-and-new-prohibitions/)

## Long Reads

**Ben Thompson — "The Inference Shift."** Thompson argues agentic inference fundamentally changes the economics of compute: when no human is waiting, latency stops mattering and the optimization frontier collapses toward throughput and cost. A useful frame for reading capex announcements over the next year. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/the-inference-shift/)

**Jack Clark — "AI systems are about to start building themselves" (Import AI 455).** Clark walks through the evidence he finds most persuasive that end-to-end automated AI R&D is now underway, and what that compresses on timelines. Worth reading alongside, not instead of, your usual skepticism. [Import AI](https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-455-automating-ai-research)

**Ben Thompson — "Data Center Discontent, Understanding the Opposition, Fixing the Problem."** A pragmatic take on the rising NIMBY backlash against hyperscaler buildout: Thompson's prescription is unsentimental — just pay communities off — and the piece is most interesting where it characterizes what the opposition actually wants. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/data-center-discontent-understanding-the-opposition-fixing-the-problem/)
