# Daily Briefing — November 7, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**OpenAI raises $11B at a $1.25T valuation, short of its target.** Bloomberg, via TechCrunch, reports the round is still open and may reach about $20B. In late September the target was $30B at $1.4T. The same day, Bloomberg reported Anthropic's expected IPO valuation has slipped to roughly $1.1T–$1.3T, down from talk of about $2T. Both numbers came out a day after Thursday's chip-led selloff. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/openai-reportedly-raises-an-additional-11-billion-at-a-1-25-trillion-valuation/)

**Ramp data: business AI spend flattened in late summer.** Spend on tokens grew just 1.5% across August and September, after 42% growth in July. Token volume kept rising over the same months. October bounced back 8.9%. TechCrunch puts the stall down to OpenAI and Anthropic price cuts and to customers getting better at controlling costs. More usage at lower prices is good for buyers. For labs about to IPO, it's an awkward chart. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/ramp-data-shows-ai-spending-stalling-just-as-anthropic-nears-its-ipo/)

**OpenAI ships ChatGPT Cyber for offensive security researchers.** Only the top tier of its Daybreak Red program gets access. OpenAI says the same model found 948 possible Linux kernel issues and 81 new Firefox vulnerabilities. It also announced $1B for critical-infrastructure defenders. One Daybreak Blue user told TechCrunch the model was recently made less useful for finding bugs. This fits Rao's "Black October" point from yesterday: the bottleneck is patching, not finding bugs. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/openai-released-a-new-cybersecurity-model-is-that-enough/)

**800+ copies of the Artex attack tool were open to the web.** Censys found the deployments running with no password. Artex is an open-source autonomous pentesting agent. Since its July release it has been linked to several attacks, and it now says it will go closed-source. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/hundreds-of-artex-ai-hacking-tools-found-publicly-exposed-to-the-web/)

**Palo Alto Networks is in talks to buy Clutch for $500M.** Clutch secures AI agents. Its ARR is near $10M, so the price is about 50x ARR. It was valued at $85M in January 2025. Neither company commented. Agent security is now a buyer's market for incumbents. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/palo-alto-networks-in-talks-to-buy-ai-agent-security-startup-clutch-for-500m/)

**Google takes over Crusoe's stalled Wyoming data center.** Crusoe confirmed the sale of the unfinished campus. Google had raised concerns about its cost and timeline, and plans to invest $17B there. The price wasn't disclosed. Crusoe is still building a separate campus for Google near Amarillo, Texas. Hyperscalers are pulling builds in-house while ballot fights make new sites harder to win. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/crusoe-sells-troubled-wyoming-data-center-project-to-google/)

**Meta adds "Spaces" to Muse, and Amazon answers with Alexa texting.** Spaces are sidebar threads where a Muse agent works toward a set goal, such as a tracker, planner or project board. Spaces can connect to outside apps and update in the background. Amazon has already blocked Muse from shopping on its site, and now lets users text Alexa for shopping help. The Amazon item is from the headline only. This is the agent-commerce fight from Thursday's Fix, now playing out in product. [Meta Spaces](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/meta-introduces-spaces-a-new-way-to-collaborate-with-your-muse-agent/) · [Alexa](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/06/after-blocking-muse-amazon-now-lets-users-text-alexa-to-help-them-shop/)
