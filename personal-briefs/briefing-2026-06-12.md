# Daily Briefing — June 12, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**OpenAI acquires Ona to beef up Codex.** OpenAI is buying Ona (the former Gitpod) for its secure, customer-controlled cloud execution environments, so Codex agents can run long jobs for hours or days without staying tethered to your laptop. OpenAI says Codex now has 5M+ weekly users, up 400% this year, with knowledge workers the fastest-growing segment. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/open-ai-ona-acquisition-codex.html)

**SpaceX prices the largest IPO ever.** SpaceX priced its IPO at $135/share on June 11, raising ~$75B at a ~$1.77T valuation — the biggest initial public offering on record. A milestone for both Musk's empire and the broader appetite for hard-tech megacaps. [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/06/11/top-tech-news-today-june-11-2026/)

**Bezos's Prometheus valued at $41B.** Jeff Bezos's AI startup Prometheus raised a round valuing it at $41B, with $12B in fresh capital from JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, and BlackRock — a striking valuation for a company still largely pre-product. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/jeff-bezos-ai-startup-valued-at-41-billion-in-funding-round)

**OpenAI models land in Oracle Cloud.** Enterprises can now access OpenAI's frontier models and Codex through existing Oracle Universal Credits, removing a separate procurement step for OCI customers. Another sign of the distribution land-grab as labs chase enterprise wallets. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/news/)

**Meta cuts 8,000 jobs while hiring into AI.** Meta is laying off roughly 8,000 people while moving 7,000 into AI teams, against a projected $125–145B in 2026 capex aimed mostly at data centers and model training. The clearest illustration yet of the industry-wide reallocation from headcount to compute. [Asanify](https://asanify.com/blog/news/industrial-physics-ai-june-11-2026/)

**EU forces Meta to reopen WhatsApp to AI assistants.** The European Commission's June 9 interim measures require Meta to restore free WhatsApp Business API access for third-party general-purpose assistants like those from OpenAI and Anthropic, reversing Meta's earlier lockout. A notable early test of how antitrust enforcement shapes who gets to plug into messaging platforms. [Build Fast with AI](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-june-11-2026)

**Bipartisan federal AI bill takes shape.** Reps. Obernolte (R-CA) and Trahan (D-MA) released a discussion draft of the Great American AI Act, the first serious attempt at a comprehensive federal framework — covering frontier governance, workforce, and cybersecurity, and proposing a three-year preemption of state laws like Colorado's, which takes effect June 30. [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/03/white-house-releases-a-national-policy-framework-for-artificial)

**Microsoft ships seven in-house MAI models.** Microsoft released a family of seven home-grown models in early June, including MAI-Thinking-1 (reasoning), MAI-Code-1-Flash, and MAI-Image-2.5 (now ranked above Gemini in image Arena). A clear bid to reduce dependence on OpenAI across the stack. [Microsoft AI](https://microsoft.ai/news/building-a-hillclimbing-machine-launching-seven-new-mai-models/)

[The Algorithmic Bridge](https://www.thealgorithmicbridge.com/p/the-three-best-pieces-of-writing)

Dense but worth it for the economic framing. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/ai-and-the-human-condition/)

**The Adolescence of Technology.** Dario Amodei's essay lays out what he sees as feasible goals for 2026 in training and steering AI systems, and the mix of methods he thinks the field needs — useful as a window into how Anthropic's CEO frames the near-term trajectory. [darioamodei.com](https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology)
