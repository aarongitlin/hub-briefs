# Daily Briefing — June 24, 2026

## From Your Inbox

**via Lenny's Newsletter:** [The new inner game: Your unfair advantage in the age of AI](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/the-new-inner-game-your-unfair-advantage) — Lenny argues that when knowledge and effort become nearly free, the scarce, learnable edge is *emotional clarity*: the ability to feel what you're feeling without being run by it. Framed as a playbook for the biggest upheaval of work we'll see in our lifetime.

**via HeyDesigner:** [The UI is still not the point](https://marieclairedean.substack.com/p/the-ui-is-still-not-the-point) — Marie-Claire Dean pushes back on the "can the machine build my UI?" framing, arguing the more interesting question is what design is *for* once generating the interface is cheap.

**via HeyDesigner:** [Atlassian's DESIGN.md is here](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/ai-at-work/atlassians-design-md-is-here-what-we-learned-testing-portable-design-context-in-practice) — Atlassian's experiment with a portable, machine-readable design-context file (a `DESIGN.md` analogous to a repo's README) so AI agents inherit design intent instead of guessing at it.

## Perspective

**via Venkatesh Rao (Contraptions):** [Search, Discovery, Pills, and Portals](https://contraptions.venkateshrao.com/p/search-discovery-pills-and-portals) — A sharp dismantling of the search-vs-discovery binary: most "discovery" engines aren't introducing genuinely novel desires, just accelerating the recognition of latent ones via *mimetic adjacency*. Rao distinguishes real novelty ("portals") from identity-reshaping "pills," and argues even pills mostly select among selves you already had.

**via The Product Model (#298):** [What Teams Risk Losing To AI](https://www.uxdx.com) — A roundup orbiting one anxiety: cognitive surrender. Lead piece, "Social Loafing: Why Groups Kill Individual Effort," reminds us teams perform when individual contribution stays visible — a useful frame as AI makes it easy to outsource the thinking and blur who actually did what.

## Long Reads

**[How we'll fight the platform war against Big AI](https://www.anildash.com/2026/06/23/fight-ai-platform-war/)** — Anil Dash (June 23). The anti-monopoly platform fight was largely lost in the last tech cycle, but Dash argues the AI market is early enough — and public sentiment hostile enough — that incumbents can still lose, *if* everyone else relearns the near-extinct craft of platform strategy rather than just venting righteous rage.

**[From AGI to ASI](https://deepmind.google/research/publications/239142/)** — Google DeepMind (mid-June). A research roadmap mapping four overlapping routes past human-level AI — scaling, algorithmic breakthroughs, recursive self-improvement, and large-scale agent coordination. Notably anti-dramatic: it argues against a singular "singularity" moment and insists even a superintelligence stays bound by hard limits (light speed, compute energy, experiment time, unsolved math).
