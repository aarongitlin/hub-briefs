## Perspective

**via John Cutler:** TBM 434: How Maps Can Hide Problems — in coherent orgs, one map (teams, goals, funding) lets you infer the others; in incoherent ones, every layer cuts differently and navigation becomes a specialized skill. His warning for the AI age: AI can surface and compare the maps but can't make conflicting structures line up — and when it smooths over contradictions, it produces the *appearance* of alignment while worsening the underlying tension. Don't fall in love with the map; use it to refactor what you see. [Read it](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-434-how-maps-can-hide-problems)

## AI & Tech News

**AI regulation day: EU AI Act fully applicable, California SB 942 operative.** August 2 is the big enforcement date on both continents — the EU AI Act's full framework kicked in with the AI Office and member states now supervising and enforcing, while California's AI Transparency Act requires gen-AI providers with 1M+ CA users to embed C2PA provenance in generated media, offer a free detection tool, and support visible AI labels ($5K/day per violation). [EU](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai) · [SB 942](https://www.ailawsbystate.com/blog/california-ai-transparency-act-sb-942)

**White House misses its own frontier-AI framework deadline.** The Aug 1 deadline in EO 14409 passed with no Federal Register notices, NIST/CISA publications, or OSTP statement on the promised benchmarking process and disclosure framework — leaving labs without a definition of "covered frontier model" and reportedly holding release timelines. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/white-house-ai-framework-deadline-002011007.html)
