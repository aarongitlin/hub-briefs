# Daily Briefing — June 3, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic confidentially files for IPO at a $965B valuation.** Hot on the heels of a $65B round that pushed it past OpenAI as the most valuable AI startup, Anthropic submitted a confidential S-1 on June 1 — potentially the largest AI listing ever and a possible fall debut. Reported revenue run-rate hit ~$47B in May, up from ~$10B a year prior. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/01/anthropic-confidentially-files-ipo-965-billion-valuation/)

**Microsoft and Google move directly against Anthropic and OpenAI on coding models.** The two incumbents are pushing their own agentic-coding offerings, the segment where Claude Code has run ahead of the field. Notably, Sundar Pichai conceded Google is "a bit behind" on agentic coding, tool use, and long-horizon tasks. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/01/microsoft-and-google-take-on-anthropic-and-openai-in-ai-coding-models.html)

**EU proposes a tech sovereignty package and a Cloud & AI Development Act.** On June 3 the European Commission unveiled measures to bolster Europe's digital autonomy, including CADA, aimed at strengthening the bloc's cloud and AI infrastructure, investment, and ecosystem. A bid to close the capacity gap with the US and China. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)

**DeepSeek nears a $7.4B raise — one of China's largest startup rounds.** Backers reportedly include Tencent and the state-backed National AI Industry Investment Fund, a sign Beijing is consolidating capital behind its open-source frontier-model champion. [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/deepseek-nears-deal-to-raise-7-4-billion-for-open-source-ai/)

**Claude Opus 4.8 tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.** Released May 28, it leads the composite index at 61.4 (ahead of GPT-5.5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and Grok 4.3) and posts 88.6% on SWE-bench Verified — a wide coding margin over rivals, though Gemini still leads on reasoning/data analysis. [llm-stats](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**EU AI Act Scientific Panel staffs up with 60 independent experts.** Announced June 1, the panel is meant to give enforcement real technical teeth as the Act's high-risk obligations phase in — though the recent Digital Omnibus pushed several Annex III deadlines to December 2027. [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-scientific-panel)

**Anthropic launches a Services Track and Partner Hub for its Partner Network.** A channel/ecosystem play to scale enterprise deployments of Claude through certified consultancies and integrators — the unglamorous plumbing that tends to precede serious enterprise revenue. [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news)

## Long Reads

**Zvi Mowshowitz — "AI #169: New Knowledge."** The latest in Zvi's exhaustive weekly roundup, covering a frontier-risk report for Feb–Mar 2026 and the recurring question of why frontier LLMs haven't "gone haywire" despite predictions. Dense but the best single survey of the week in AI. [The Zvi](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/ai-169-new-knowledge)

**"The Three Best Pieces of Writing About AI in 2026."** Alberto Romero rounds up the take-off-scenario writing currently circulating (Shumer's "Something Big Is Happening," Citrini's "2028 Global Intelligence Crisis," and Amodei's "Adolescence of Technology") — useful as a map of the genre, with the caveat that he frames most of these as speculative fiction rather than forecasts. [The Algorithmic Bridge](https://www.thealgorithmicbridge.com/p/the-three-best-pieces-of-writing)

**NBER — "Data Centers and Local Economies in the Age of AI."** A new working paper (Alvarez, Argente, Chow, Van Patten) uses a shift-share approach to quantify what a data center actually does to a local economy: capital and construction in, but pressure on power, land, and grid out. Grounding for the year's loudest infrastructure debate. [NBER](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35194)
