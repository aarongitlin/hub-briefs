# Weekly Brief — Oct 26–Nov 1, 2026

The ambient layer is growing inside the platforms that already own the shopper, not through outside agents: Amazon's own assistant passed 400M users while DoorDash counted third-party agent orders in the hundreds. On the design side, the week's best writing named who owns agent workflows, and found design missing from the list.

## Commerce × Media × AI

- **Native assistants are scaling. Third-party shopping agents are still a rounding error.** Amazon says 400M+ customers used Alexa for Shopping (the renamed Rufus) in the last 12 months, up from 350M in July, and that users spend 50% more. That 50% is Jassy's own number with no stated method, so it likely reflects who opts in. The same week, DoorDash's Tony Xu said third-party agents like Muse have placed "maybe several hundred orders in total," against about 3M deliveries a day. For a shopping-ads team, the assistant inside the platform is where agent-mediated buying actually happens today. [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/amazon-says-more-than-400m-customers-have-used-alexa-for-shopping-in-the-last-12-months/) · [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/doordash-said-its-only-received-several-hundred-orders-from-ai-agents/)
- **Google said out loud that AI Mode earns less than classic search.** Search revenue growth went from 19% in Q1 to 17% in Q2 to 12% in Q3 ($63.35B, flat on Q2), and Google tied the slowdown to strong AI Mode query growth with "lower monetization." Alphabet stock fell about 12%, its worst day ever. Google is responding by scaling AI Mode ads to 10 more countries and launching multiturn ads in the US. Ad formats that work inside conversations are now a revenue problem, not a side project. [Relevant Audience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/google-ads-en/alphabet-q3-2026-search-revenue-ai-mode-monetization/)
- **Instagram is coming for YouTube on the TV.** "Features," a long-form section for horizontal video up to 90 minutes, is testing with US creators, and short cuts of the videos feed them through Reels. Instagram for TV is now on Apple TV in the US. Meta has no TV platform of its own, so installs are the hurdle. Still, creator video is the inventory shoppable formats run on, and this goes straight at it. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/10/26/instagram-long-videos-features-mosseri/)

## UX Practice × Agentic AI

- **Every function is building its own agent "factory," and design is between them.** John Cutler argues engineers are moving up to own orchestration, quality gates, and evals. PM, go-to-market, and content are each doing the same, and each one stops at its own professional border. His line: "poor design is sitting somewhere in the middle wondering WTF is going on." The gaps between those factories are where the user experience lives, and right now nobody owns them. [The Beautiful Mess](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-448-the-software-factory-as-identity)
- **Reddit's in-house prototyping tool has made ~700 prototypes, and designers never touch Git.** Blair Metcalf's Reddit Proto runs on real accounts and data. A custom design-system MCP (a plug-in that gives the AI tool access to tokens and components) maps them to production code, so the coding agent picks the right component. His heaviest users are a visual designer and an animator. Here, the design system becomes the agent's instruction set. [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-i-ai-claude-duet-honest-review)
- **New since last week: Claude Duet's approval UX is good, and people still forget to open it.** Claire Vo calls Duet the most polished personal-agent launch yet, then dropped it. Parallel tasks "routinely took more than 10 minutes" with little feedback, and its quiet notifications gave her no reason to come back. Her point is that latency is a product problem. Progress and re-engagement need design, not just permission. [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-i-ai-claude-duet-honest-review)

## Thesis check
- **Commerce:** Complicated. Agent-mediated shopping is real at scale (400M Alexa for Shopping users), but almost entirely inside the store's own assistant. DoorDash's numbers say open, cross-platform agents barely buy anything yet.
- **UX practice:** Strengthened, with a warning. Reddit Proto and Duet both put the value in system definition and behavior, but Cutler's point is that design may not be in the room where those systems get defined.
