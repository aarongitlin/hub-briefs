## AI & Tech News

**Signal limits screenshots on iOS and Android to block OS-level AI agents.** Screenshots were already blocked by default on Windows. Meredith Whittaker calls AI agents that read your screen an "existential threat" to private messaging. Users can turn the block off, which she says breaks Signal's privacy guarantees. She calls the fix "incomplete." This is a direct clash with the screen-reading agents Apple, Google and Microsoft are all shipping. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/21/signal-restricts-mobile-screenshots-as-it-confronts-existential-threat-of-ai-surveillance/)

**Nvidia's week: record revenue, a state subpoena and a petition to bring Jensen back.** California AG Rob Bonta served Nvidia with an investigative subpoena on Nov 19, tied to the Huang scandal and whether investors were misled about China risk. Interim CEO Colette Kress says Nvidia will stop selling H200s in China. Elon Musk is pushing a petition to reinstate Huang, and the board still says no. Critics claim about 20% of Nvidia's business reaches China indirectly through Malaysia and other countries, but that number is unverified. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/21/a-tale-of-two-nvidias/) · [Reuters via Daily Guardian](https://thedailyguardian.com/business/california-ag-subpoenas-nvidia-over-concerns-about-former-ceo-china-ties-776375/)

**Oregon sues OpenAI for $2.035B over teen safety on ChatGPT.** AG Dan Rayfield filed on Nov 17. The state claims OpenAI knowingly put teens at risk and misled the public. It follows California's runaway-agents suit and October's multistate case, so state AGs are now OpenAI's main regulators in practice. [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/11/17/oregon-ag-sues-openai-claims-company-knowingly-endangers-teens/) · [Engadget](https://engadget.com/2320041/oregon-is-the-latest-state-to-sue-openai-over-child-safety)

**A microdrama startup premieres a fully AI series built on one actor's licensed face.** Shortical's "Inevitable" stars an avatar of actor Aki Avni. It's the company's first face-licensing deal. ## Long Reads

**Ben Thompson interviews Eric Seufert on agentic commerce (Nov 19).** This is the most direct look yet at how ad-funded commerce changes when an agent does the shopping, and who gets paid when no one sees the ad. It may be paywalled. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-eric-seufert-about-agentic-commerce/)

**Graham Dumpleton: "Muru: when lazy imports help" (Nov 22).** Python 3.15's lazy imports (PEP 810) speed up command-line tools but can slow down a web server that forks worker processes. Muru, an AI-assisted Rust server for Python apps, measures imports to decide what to defer and what to preload. It's a good, concrete case study of building serious infrastructure with an agent.
