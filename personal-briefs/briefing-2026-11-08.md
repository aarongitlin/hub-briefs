## Perspective

**via John Cutler, TBM 450 — "The Loss of Positive Friction":** "The bottleneck has moved" is too simple. The slow part of the process was also an enabling constraint: pairing, rounds of doc revision, and "wait, is that what they meant?" moments. Going from a customer call to a deployed feature in an hour skips those checks. As people retreat into single-player agent workflows, teams have to make room for that friction on purpose. [The Beautiful Mess](https://cutlefish.substack.com/p/tbm-450-the-loss-of-positive-friction)

**via John Cutler — "Don't Let Layoff Narratives Rewrite Your Reality":** Cutler compares two kinds of layoff story. In the first, the company names a crisis and the whole team struggles with it. In the second, years of "things are fine" end with a turn to "AI-native, flatter, higher bar." That second story lets leaders look in control without admitting what went wrong. Airbnb is his example: its 2020 layoffs were framed one way, and the later "Founder Mode" framing the other. His advice: don't judge yourself or your colleagues by the story leadership needs to tell. [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/dont-let-layoff-narratives-rewrite-your-reality-john-cutler-l4unc)
