# Daily Briefing — May 26, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**Pope Leo XIV releases first encyclical, *Magnifica Humanitas*, on AI.** Presented May 25 alongside Anthropic co-founder Chris Olah, the document frames AI as "the Industrial Revolution of our time" and positions the Catholic Church as a moral stakeholder in how it's developed — a notable signal of how mainstream the ethics conversation has become. [JDSupra summary](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/ai-today-in-5-may-26-2026-the-tower-o-79745/)

**Apple quietly registers a "gen AI" subdomain ahead of WWDC.** Spotted on May 23, the move is the latest tell that Apple plans a substantial generative-AI push at the June 8 keynote, with reporting pointing to a long-delayed Siri 2.0, a standalone Siri app, and the option to pick Gemini, ChatGPT, or Claude as a default model. [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/05/23/apple-new-gen-ai-subdomain-ahead-of-wwdc/) · [eWeek preview](https://www.eweek.com/news/apple-wwdc-2026-ai-preview/)

**Nvidia posts a record $58.3B quarterly profit — and concedes China to Huawei.** Revenue hit $81.6B (up 85% YoY) on continued AI-chip demand, but Jensen Huang said publicly that Nvidia has "largely conceded" China's advanced-AI market after April export-license rules effectively shut the company out. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/5/21/nvidia-posts-record-profit-and-revenue-amid-ai-chip-boom) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/21/nvidia-jensen-huang-china-ai-chip-market-huawei.html)

**Anthropic closes a second $30B raise of 2026 at a >$900B valuation.** Led by Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks, the round comes as Anthropic's annualized revenue jumped from $14B in February to $30B in April. The company also acqui-hired Stainless — less for the product than for the SDK-generation pipes that increasingly shape how developers touch AI APIs. [StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-news/2026/four-labs-four-acquisitions-ai-consolidation-may-2026)

**Google I/O leans cheap-and-fast: Gemini 3.5 Flash GA, AI Ultra cut to $100/mo.** The new Flash claims frontier-level intelligence at 4x the speed of comparable models for $1.50/$9 per 1M tokens, scores 76.2% on Terminal-Bench 2.1, and reportedly beats Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding/agents. The reshuffled $100 Ultra tier (down from $250) bundles 5x Gemini limits, 20TB storage, YouTube Premium, and Gemini Spark beta access. [llm-stats updates](https://llm-stats.com/llm-updates)

**Google DeepMind absorbs the Contextual AI team via an $80–90M "licensing" deal.** Structured to avoid antitrust review as a merger, the arrangement continues the now-familiar acqui-hire pattern that's quietly hollowed out the mid-tier of US AI startups. Worth tracking how the FTC eventually responds. [StartupHub](https://www.startuphub.ai/ai-news/ai-news/2026/four-labs-four-acquisitions-ai-consolidation-may-2026)

**EU Council and Parliament cut a deal to simplify the AI Act.** With high-risk-system provisions due to take force August 2, 2026, negotiators agreed on a streamlining package that keeps the law's core thrust intact while reducing compliance friction — a modest but real concession to industry pressure. [Consilium press release](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/05/07/artificial-intelligence-council-and-parliament-agree-to-simplify-and-streamline-rules/)
