# Daily Briefing — May 25, 2026

## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic signals eventual public release of Mythos-class models.** In a May 22 update, Anthropic said it intends to make Mythos-class models broadly available "once we've developed the far stronger safeguards we need" — notable because the model has been used internally and via Project Glasswing to surface thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities, including ~2,000 bugs in Cloudflare's critical path. No timeline was given. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/05/25/anthropic-to-release-mythos-class-models-to-the-public/5245596)

**Anthropic closing a $30B+ round at a ~$900B valuation.** Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks are reportedly co-leading, with the round expected to close imminently — coming on top of the recently announced $40B add-on from Google and $5B from Amazon. The cap table is starting to look like a sovereign wealth fund. [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week)

**Google opens MCP to third-party agents in Gemini Spark.** Following last week's I/O, Google confirmed Gemini Spark will get MCP support for partners like Canva, Instacart, and OpenTable in the coming weeks, and the new Managed Agents API lets a single call spin up a stateful agent. Looks like a deliberate play to make Gemini the agent runtime layer rather than just another chat surface. [Build Fast with AI recap](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/ai-news-today-may-23-2026)

**Microsoft ships "computer use" agents to GA in Copilot Studio.** Enterprises can now deploy agents that visually navigate apps, forms, and legacy systems instead of relying on APIs or brittle RPA scripts. This is the enterprise-grade version of the browser-use pattern OpenAI and Anthropic have been demoing for a year. [Agile Brand Guide](https://agilebrandguide.com/yesterdays-marketing-technology-ai-news-may-23-2026/)

**Sierra raises $950M at a $15B+ valuation.** Bret Taylor's enterprise-AI agent platform, led by Tiger Global and GV — the latest data point that capital is consolidating around a handful of agent platforms targeting customer-facing workflows. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/04/sierra-raises-950m-as-the-race-to-own-enterprise-ai-gets-serious/)

**Meta hikes 2026 capex to $125B–$145B, blames memory pricing.** The company attributes most of the increase to "higher component pricing this year, particularly memory" — a reminder that AI data centers now consume roughly 70% of global memory output and that the squeeze is showing up in every hyperscaler's guidance. Meta also detailed its in-house MTIA 400/450/500 chip line. [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/big-tech/microsoft-attributed-25-billion-of-its-record-ai-budget-to-memory-chip-costs)

**Trump White House reportedly weighing AI oversight reversal.** Driven specifically by Mythos-class capability concerns, the administration is debating policies it had previously dismissed, including pre-release vetting. Worth tracking because it would mark the first concrete federal pivot away from the "regulate as little as possible" stance. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/05/06/trump-administration-embraces-ai-oversight-policies-it-once-rejected-anthropic-mythos-caisi/)

## Long Reads

**State of AI: May 2026 (Air Street).** Nathan Benaich's monthly synthesis is the cleanest single read on where the frontier sits this month — frontier pricing down 30–60%, open-weights within 5–15 points of closed, and a widening Western/Chinese pricing gap of 5–25x at equivalent benchmarks. [Air Street Press](https://press.airstreet.com/p/state-of-ai-may-2026)

**Earning & Spending — Ben Thompson on the inference-era capex bubble.** Thompson argues Amazon's perceived training-era weakness is becoming an inference-era advantage, and uses it as a lens on which hyperscaler bets actually pay back. Useful framing if you're trying to make sense of why everyone's capex is now larger than their free cash flow. [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/earning-spending/)

**Automated Weak-to-Strong Researcher (Anthropic Alignment).** Anthropic's alignment team describes a system where autonomous agents proposed, ran, and iterated on an open scalable-oversight problem — and outperformed human researchers on the metric. A concrete data point in the "AI doing AI research" arc, with all the recursive-self-improvement implications that entails. [alignment.anthropic.com](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/automated-w2s-researcher/)
