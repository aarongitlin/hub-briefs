**via Superhuman: OpenAI's OpenWorks pays AI experts up to $8,000/month to help local small businesses.** The pilot covers 20 US communities and targets restaurants, retailers and service firms. **via Superhuman: LLMs as synthetic survey panels.** A Colgate-Palmolive paper uses "Semantic Similarity Rating." The model answers in prose, and the prose is then mapped to a score. On 9,300 real responses, it reached about 90% of human test-retest reliability, but only when prompted with the persona's demographics. Simile ($2B valuation) and Aaru are building businesses on the same idea. Note: the paper is from October 2025, so Superhuman is repackaging older work. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.08338)

**via HeyDesigner: Building your own tools is now part of the craft.** Luke Wroblewski's "Design by playground" says a frontier model with a good harness can spin up an interactive playground in minutes for pennies, so you explore by building, not by mocking up. Marie-Claire Dean makes the same case in "Design tools just got personal." Anton Sten adds the counterweight: when continuing a style becomes nearly free, the starting point matters more. ## AI & Tech News

**Anthropic posts a Claude-authored proof that VP ≠ VNP, not yet checked by any human.** VP vs VNP is the algebraic cousin of P vs NP. The 70-page paper lists "Claude (Anthropic)" as sole author and comes with a Lean 4 formalization of about 73,000 lines. It lands days after the Riemann claim. Anthropic's own README is careful: Lean checks the proof, but nothing checks that the formal statement matches the intended theorem. That gap is exactly Evans's "can we understand it" question from yesterday. No substantive expert review exists yet. [GitHub](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/tree/main/vp-vnp) · [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/ai/claude-vp-vnp-proof-valiant-conjecture-p-vs-np/)
