## AI & Tech News

**Gemini can now drive your Mac, and Apple is building its own agent.** Gemini's macOS app gained computer use: it operates native apps and browses in the background, and you can pause, stop or approve its actions. It needs a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra plan and is US/English only for now. Bloomberg says Apple is hiring to build an agent that uses the computer, possibly by end of next year. macOS 27.3 will also warn users before they grant third-party agents full access, which is relevant if you give agents on the mini broad permissions. [TechCrunch: Gemini](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/google-gives-gemini-the-ability-to-use-your-mac-for-you/) · [TechCrunch: Apple](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/apple-may-be-developing-its-own-ai-agent-that-runs-your-mac/)

**Meta's Muse agent is reportedly leaking other users' data.** Reuters, citing internal posts, says employees saw Muse mix up conversations across accounts and show other people's messages and account balances. Muse launched in September and has more than 10M downloads. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/metas-ai-agent-muse-is-mixing-up-conversations-and-exposing-private-data-report-says/)

**A wave of big outages, and AI-speed vulnerability discovery gets the blame.** Apple Pay, NASA's internal network and Microsoft's Outlook/Teams/Copilot all went down; the Microsoft outage lasted nearly two days. The FAA grounded all US flights for hours. Security people blame AI surfacing bugs faster than legacy systems can be patched. The DNI says the outages don't look state-sponsored. PostgreSQL 19 also shipped about two months late, mostly because of a flood of AI-reported vulnerabilities. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/19/these-tech-outages-are-giving-y2k-ai-agent-style/) · [Simon Willison on Postgres 19](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/19/postgresql-19/)

**Token fraud is now a real cost for AI startups.** Claire Vo (ChatPRD) says organized groups abuse free trials and promo codes to resell tokens or run commercial work on her agent. She is ending free trials entirely. [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/20/token-fraud-is-here-and-its-bad/)
