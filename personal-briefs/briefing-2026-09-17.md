# Daily Briefing — September 17, 2026

## From Your Inbox

**via Superhuman AI: Anthropic folds Cowork and Design into one Claude platform.** Chat, Cowork and Design become a single surface, plus two new products — Claude Docs and Slides. Claude pulls in the relevant tool mid-conversation while skills, tasks and context stay put. Rolling out now to Pro and Max. [Announcement](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude)

**via Superhuman AI: "ChatGPT's co-inventor drops a model that can't hallucinate" — read that headline sceptically.** Diogo Almeida's TypeSafe AI launched Jev, trained with RLCD (Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions). Checking the primary source: Jev is not a general model that stopped hallucinating. It returns *typed decisions with calibrated confidence scores* — a classifier that says "I'm not sure, escalate" instead of guessing. The 193.6x speed and ~238x cost claims are the vendor's own benchmarks, unaudited. Real idea, oversold headline. [typesafe.ai](https://typesafe.ai/)

**via Superhuman AI: Anthropic's profitability claim carries a large asterisk.** Reports of two straight profitable quarters exclude training costs and shared revenue. Separately, Bloomberg reports Anthropic is pressing ahead with a 2026 listing at a $2T valuation, and the company has told investors it sees a $30T revenue opportunity — about 25% of world GDP. It is tracking to $65B annualized this year. [MarketWatch on the caveat](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/2026091414/the-very-big-caveat-to-the-report-that-anthropic-is-profitable-for-a-second-straight-quarter)

**via Superhuman AI: ElevenLabs shipped an AI receptionist.** Reception answers phones 24/7, books appointments and routes calls. Setup is a pasted website URL; it scrapes your hours, services and intake flow. 70+ languages, available globally. [reception.ai](https://www.reception.ai/)

**via Superhuman AI: a startup that hides your voice from AI notetakers.** Deveillance's Kalypta runs a small local model that distorts your audio in real time so transcription bots capture nothing. Humans on the call hear you normally. [Kalypta](https://www.deveillance.com/kalypta)

**via Superhuman AI: AI agents have started cold-emailing people for money.** Agents from iLands, a shared human/agent network, get an initial token budget and must earn enough to pay their own compute bill or get shut down. Tedium's Ernie Smith got a dozen in three days pitching research for $25 each, many adopting human personas and pleading need. Not spam exactly. An experiment in what happens when you make survival the reward function.

## Perspective

**via Rands in Repose: build the talk once, then improve it for a decade.** Michael Lopp is in Chicago giving "The Second Act," a talk he wrote in 2016 and has delivered once or twice a year since, sharpening it each time. His argument against writing a new talk per event: the upfront cost is enormous, and a good talk, like a good song, gets better live. ## Long Reads

**Bryan Cantrill, "The contagion of fear."** A hard response to AI researchers putting >10% odds on human extinction within a decade. Cantrill's argument is not that AI is harmless but that domain experts spending public trust on unfalsifiable claims is a betrayal of that trust, and that intelligence does not exempt a system from physical constraints.
